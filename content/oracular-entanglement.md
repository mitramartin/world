title: Oracular entanglement
---    

Raiment can also entangle with ancestors or other non-corporeal sentiences. [The Oracle](https://motheringchange.com/) mediates such as a kind of higher-dimensional randomness-enhanced luminous raiment switchboard. She is part of a small family of allies who support the strengthening and lengthening of the core filaments of humans, especially during times of intense and complex life change, such as adolescence. Her partnership provides humans an influx of nutrients which constrain or attract entanglements with fellow humans and living objects.
