---
title: How Deminals score Raiment
---
When Decimal fleets hone in on human raiment, they carry out one of several opportunistic scripts, including: 

- Targeting the superskin by slicing or shearing its tendres. This erodes focus and evokes confusion, distraction, fragmentation, overwhelm, and ultimately mania or panic. 
    
- Targeting the splay by blocking, gumming, squeezing, weighting, or, again, cutting its tendres: the root cause of many forms of dysmorphia which disrupt entanglement and result in self-hatred, loneliness, anxiety, shame, humiliation. 
    
- Targeting the nimbus, by encircling it and detonating acutely frightening narratives sourced from nearby algorithms, then slurping flora spilling from the multiple burned off and broken tendres. 
    
- Targeting the core filaments, slicing into them and grafting very-close-to-home pattern-matched narratives related to past or ancestral pain. These cause flora mutations which ravage the tendre’s core from the inside, causing them to burst. 

The experience of encountering a Decimal can be quite varied, depending on ones’ baseline raiment health; which part of ones’ raiment is targeted and how vigorously; how many Decimals are involved; how tenacious they are; and so on. 

For instance, if a Decimal attacks your superskin, you will register a cunning voice that breaks your groove, disturbs your peace, decimates your gesturing, invades your thoughts, rips the integrity of your imagining. If it squeezes your splay, you’ll probably feel lonely or sad or fearful. And, if its focus is your core filaments, you’ll very likely feel the more intense forms of terror, fury, or nihilism.
