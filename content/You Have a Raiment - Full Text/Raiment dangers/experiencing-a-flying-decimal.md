title: Experiencing a Flying Decimal
---
The experience of encountering a flying Decimal can be quite varied, depending on ones’ baseline raiment health; which part of ones’ raiment is targeted and how vigorously; how many Decimals are involved; how tenacious they are; and so on. 

For instance, if a Decimal attacks your superskin, you will register a cunning voice that breaks your groove, disturbs your peace, decimates your gesturing, invades your thoughts, rips the integrity of your imagining. It tears into delicate experiences wherein, perhaps, you are attempting to thread a subtle lemma, and sucks up the juicy confusion and irritation that results. If it squeezes your splay, you’ll probably feel lonely or sad or fearful. And, if its focus is your core filaments, you’ll very likely feel the more intense forms of terror, fury, or nihilism. 

*Note: Interestingly, within the past bicentenary there lived a famous thaumaturge, a woman called Montessori, who figured out how to set up a raiment-fortification force-field where Flying Decimals were completely inoperable. Her techniques are not yet being leveraged at scale.*
