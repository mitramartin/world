title: Mom and Dad’s Fones
---
When was the last time your mom or dad went into Fone while they were talking to you or you were talking to them? What was that like? 

Sometimes moms and dads haven’t trained their Fone Callers very well. You can help! They might need a Box. A box that they can put their Fone Caller in to help them remember not to use it during dinner or when they are talking to you.

Draw a box for your mom or dad’s Fone. What color and shape and design is it? What is its name? What would you tell your mom or dad when you give it to them?
