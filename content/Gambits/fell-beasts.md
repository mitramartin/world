---
title: Fell beasts
---
Help us catalog the dangers of Fone! We are building a comprehensive crowdsourced repository of Fone monsters witnessed by tourists, to complement the official Bestiary. Please inform us of any and all fell beasts you have encountered in Fone. We welcome submissions in the form of original art: draw a picture of a Fone monster that you have met, heard of, or that you think might exist, give it a name, then please leave it in our secret Vestibule.
