title: Deliberate Raiment Cultivation
---
Perhaps of late, since you picked up this volume, you’ve been sensing the sweetness of your own growing tendres in small ways; maybe you’ve noticed those electric moments of tendre touch, or you’ve put in that extra effort to cloak. Possibly you’ve simply been a little more attentive to introductions, facing someone and pronouncing your noble name! We see you! 

Despite all the heartbreaks of recent Tales, we hope that you’re ready to learn about how to make the most of your extraordinary organ, your raiment, as are also some of the key protagonists of the above tales. 

This section will walk you through all the specific, intentional ways you can join with others for the purposes of joyous raiment entanglement and all the wonders that proceed therefrom. There are many 100% non-blingy ways to participate in excitingly new raiment culture these days.
