A thrilling modern example of a numie is Torvill & Dean’s subsumement into The Boléro which is still blooming today. 

Many musical tango integers were able to formulate numies. Some examples are the works: Bahia Blanca; À Evaristo Carriego.

Within the Tales to be Told Aloud After Feasting Together, we see the origami of many epic numies. The entire Kistler family is gifted with skills of numie-craftsmanship, whether through Arthur and his partner Dante’s Bells! Bells! musical, their children Penelope and Linus’ very tiny but perfect ringtone which formed the basis of an entirely new Kistler operating system, or even Cora Kistler’s early portrait work in collaboration with First Best & Only and later underworld works.
