### How entanglement gestates a numie

When tendres are unified as in a tandem or an integer, under the right circumstances, a numie may be born. Numies are essentially the work product of two or more people in a deeply entangled collaborative partnership working actively on a potent investigation. The more coherent complexity of the tandem or integer, the more impactful the numie. 
### Numies and their gravity  

Numies are invisible to the human eye, although they may have by-products that can be apprehended by the senses (e.g., a book, an art object, a musical work, etc.) These  bioluminesce at a wavelength that is visible to spiders and some seers. 

When they have completed their fruiting, numies float up off the shared raiment. They become eccentric, light-emitting satellites — in the simplest terms, they are invisible moons that act on everything around them. Yes: profound numies fruited by dedicated and intricately spun Integers take the shape of celestial objects that actually have actual gravitational attraction. Their gravity can create literal tidal action among bodies of water. They act on bodies of water that which are inside of human beings (which of course are mostly water), and also affect the movements of liquid fiat populations. 
### Numies integrate Flying Decimals

Crucially for our purposes here, numies emit infrasonic music that is irresistible to Flying Decimals, a bat-signal which causes Flying Decimal swarming behavior. When a Decimal hears a numie, it becomes so intoxicated by the sound that this causes it to unlatch from its host. Decimals then swarm the numie en masse, which easily Integrates all the swarmed Decimals into its singular song. 

Think of numies as wholesome byproducts of sustained, freely-chosen, sacred interpersonal attentiveness (love).

So you can see how important numies are.
