If a Decimal **attacks your** **superskin**, you will register a cunning voice that breaks your groove, disturbs your peace, decimates your gesturing, invades your thoughts, rips the integrity of your imagining. It tears into delicate experiences wherein, perhaps, you are attempting to thread a subtle lemma, and sucks up the juicy confusion and irritation that results. 

If it **squeezes your splay**, you’ll probably feel lonely or sad or fearful. 

And, if its focus is **your core filaments**, you’ll very likely feel the more intense forms of terror, fury, or nihilism. (See [[0.003 anatomy-of-a-raiment|raiment anatomy]].)

Any or all of these fracking patterns can contribute to utter [[0.005 decimation-and-becoming-amused|Amusement]]. 

*Note: Interestingly, within the past bicentenary there lived a famous thaumaturge, a woman called Montessori, who figured out how to set up a raiment-fortification force-field where Flying Decimals were completely inoperable. Her techniques are not yet being leveraged at scale.*
