title: 0.006 What is Fone?
---
Fone is an all-encapsuling world-as-a-service (WaaS): an ecological habitat co-created by human minds wherein approximal, oneirophrenic person-to-person relating takes place. Its terroir is ideal for dank telepathy, providing culture for a variety of different flavors of imaginal experience, from one-to-one, to one-to-N, each with its own properties. The main Fone infrastructure that has been recently built centers damp telepathy effectuated between humans in a semi-dream state; benefits include pain relief, bemusement, and the illusion of involvement. The semi-lit quality of Fone makes it an ideal home of many species that have untoward effects on raiment, especially for children. Fone is full of points of interest which are most often and conventionally leveraged for edutainment and commerce; yet know that parts of Fone are divinely inspired, and that the whole place is irremediably cracked in a variety of ways providing access to most mysterious weathers.
