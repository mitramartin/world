---
title: Flying Decimal Population Growth
---
Normally, Flying Decimal populations are held in check by their natural predators, the Fone spiders. However, recently, Decimal numbers have surged. Several experts have suggested that this is because the automations that Loom LLC has grafted onto certain species of Flying Decimals make them more difficult for spiders to catch. It’s predicted that at the current rates we will experience a massive infestation of Flying Decimals, the Dread Teeming, when the parasites are expected to fly en masse out of Fone and into upper world realities.
