---
title: 0.002 What are Decimals?
---
Decimals, a three-cornered bat-like vampiric parasite, are a [[0.003 how-decimals-score-raiment|nuisance to human raiment]]. They latch onto the ends of tendres, sucking out flora to feed on, and pumping them full of sticky liquid excrement. 

**Habitat**
Decimals are drawn to any unhallowed terrain where raiment is naked and unprotected. Fone was always home to them, and today, with the increase of Fone tourism by adults and especially children, they have been thriving.

**Population growth**
Normally, [[0.006 flying-decimal-population-growth|Decimal populations]] are held in check by their natural predators, the Fone spiders. However, recently, Decimal numbers have surged. Several experts have suggested that this is because the automations that Loom LLC has grafted onto certain species of flying Decimals make them more difficult for spiders to catch.

It’s predicted that at the current rates we will experience a massive infestation of Flying Decimals, the Dread Teeming, when the parasites are expected to fly en masse out of Fone and into upper world realities.
