title: 0.005 Decimation, and becoming Amused
---
A [[0.008 raiment-preventative-care|healthy raiment]] can weather occasional attacks of [[0.002 what-are-decimals|Flying Decimals]]. Although a decimated tendre is painful, it can regrow with time and appropriate interactive somatic practices, detailed below. But if a raiment is already weak, and several tendres become entangled with heavy decimals invasively and simultaneously, it leads to a condition known as Decimation. 

*Partial Decimation* isn’t always easy to diagnose and its [symptoms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Directed_attention_fatigue) can be confused with other conditions. They include: feeling unusually distractible, impatient, forgetful, or cranky when there is no associated illness. In more severe forms, it can lead to bad judgment, apathy, or accidents, and can contribute to increased stress levels.  

*Full Decimation* occurs when there are no tendres left for human [[0.012 splay-skill-entanglement|entanglement]]. This is what is commonly known in Alexandria circles as “**becoming Amused**.” This is the worst diagnosis that we’re aware of that is correlated with [[0.006 what-is-fone|Fone]] visiting. Symptoms are behaviors that are chronically antisocial and express consistently non-affiliative humor in the form of cruel jokes and savage memes. 

People in Alexandria don’t know why kids become Amused. Most suspect bad character, bad upbringing, bad luck, forgetting to put on Fone spray before visiting. [[0.005 who-can-see-raiment|Raiment-seers]] have insight into the underlying mechanisms of how this is caused: by the atrophy and total decimation of a raiment.
