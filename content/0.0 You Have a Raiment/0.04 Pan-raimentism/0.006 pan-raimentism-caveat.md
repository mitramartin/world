---
title: 0.006 Pan-raimentism caveat
---
Pan-raimentism is at the very vanguard of raiment research, and it is exciting to have the opportunity to acquaint you with the possibilities. As you can probably tell, the authors find it very energizing to contemplate. 

We would be amiss not to also acknowledge that this module is also most speculative, because these are questions and practices of entanglement at vast scales that are tough to imagine. Nobody has practical experience in any of this yet. Thinking on a whole-world level can bring up [[0.004 experiencing-a-flying-decimal|anti-raiment agents like cyncism, laziness, hopelessness, disaffection, and distrust]]. And those who theorize at that level may be a canvas for projections of other people’s fears, and thus deemed maniacally grandiose and conveniently othered in a variety of in other ways. 

We don’t mind that, and it also makes it quite handy that we’ve chosen (for now!) to remain anonymous authors.
