Early groups of pan-raiment theorists proposed that the health of young raiments was so critically decisive to the pan-raiment project (not to mention the future of our species) that a social enterprise built intentionally around centering the raiment health of the young would be a project of the utmost value. 

Over two decades, they collaboratively developed a pattern language for the pan-health of earth’s youthful raiment, drawing on modalities as diverse as mixed-methods research, urban design, lucid dreaming, entheogens, and thaumaturgy. 

The project, code-named INTI (Is Not This I?) did not receive public acclaim but has been deeply influential for pragmatic utopianists of these times. Mehrzad Esfandiari was an active member and contributor to the INTI project, and authored several patterns in the form of his distilled “[[Notes]].”
### Core pan-raiment patterns 

The pan-raiment proposal project identified the following core patterns to support the development of a sane and wholesome universal youth raiment. These are: 

1. **The children’s corridor**. A literal place consecrated to the health of youthful raiment. 
2. **The children’ parliament**. A grassroots governance system, federated at several levels. Through it children from five to eighteen bring their voice to public endeavor while practicing consent-based decision-making and restorative circles, thus representing their raiments’ unique needs to the broader world. 
3. **Convivial living**. A pattern of 5-10 families settling in a 10-minute walking radius from one another and pooling resources for the creation of common space where integers and tandems can evolve while minimizing the need for unhallowed technologies like clocks, cars, Fone. Convivial spaces are made using age-inclusive design principles. 
4. **Polyparenting**. The practice of multiple couples and individuals choosing to collectively commit to the rearing, raiment protection and raiment cultivation of a specific child or children. 
5. **Developmental rites**. Rites-of-passage that support the child and her community in acknowledging the new capacities of her raiment at key developmental milestones and affirm the privileges and responsibilities this maturation incurs. These involve practices guided by chance operations and plant teachers. 
6. **Slow learning.** Slow learning is a practice that centers relationships, projects, and experiences in the learning paradigm, thus de-emphasizing content, activities, and measurement through grades/points/testing. The method of reifying slow learning is the concept of SlowXP, or “slow experience points.” 
7. **Interactive somatics**. Interactive somatics has been amply treated above. Cross-cultural interactive somatics fellowships like the Tango Guild operate musical integers at scale to support the maturation of the raiment of youth and their caregivers. 
8. **Onboarding**. This pattern relates to the practice of asserting alertness in the face of hot, new, unhallowed places like Fone. It calls for such technologies to be metabolized carefully and introduced with special attentiveness to their impacts on the vast raiment complex.
