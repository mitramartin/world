### What is pan-raiment?

Pan-raiment is unified orchestrated uber entanglement of utter gentleness; the invisible hand of which we are all fingers; the great branching trunk of which we are all floral tips. 

Achieving pan-raiment would mean that each individual human raiment is participant to at least one [[0.004 tanda-first-stage-of-entanglement|tandem]] and one [[0.006 integer-third-stage-of-entanglement|integer]] at all times, and affiliated with a Placed [[0.001 introduction-to-fiats|fiat]]. 

This would be actualized through a system for cultivating integers at scale. It is a pragmatic, bottom-up vision for terrestrial paradise. Pan-raiment is the end-state goal of the loose federation of interpersonal somatics labs. 
#### Inter-species entanglements

Some enthusiasts propose that pan-raimentism should not just include [[0.012 splay-skill-entanglement|human-to-human entanglements]]. They suggest that for a truly durable pan-raiment would require entangling with non-human persons, such as plant and animal friends.

Some researchers maintain that raiment can entangle not only with organic beings but also with ancestors and [[0.014 oracular-entanglement|other non-corporeal sentiences]] — and that these entanglements provide an influx of nutrients that support humans’ core filaments especially during times of intense and complex life change, such as adolescence. 
#### Object-human entanglements

Perhaps less known is the possibility of entangling with less obviously animate beings. Many traditions have accurately sensed that certain storied objects (e.g., numies) have living tendres, such as doorknobs or fire utensils. What’s less common is to notice the raiment of any old object. A very small but passionate group of universal pan-raimentists hold that literally everything has at least one tendre and thus the capacity to entangle, and that a true pan-raiment should strive to exclude nothing, not even, for instance, a cracked bell or a broken teapot.
