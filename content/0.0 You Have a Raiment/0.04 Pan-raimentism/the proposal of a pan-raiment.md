### What is a pan-raiment?

Pan-raiment is unified orchestrated uber entanglement of utter gentleness; the invisible hand of which we are all fingers; the great branching trunk of which we are all floral tips. 

Achieving pan-raiment would mean that each individual human raiment is participant to at least one tandem and one integer at all times, and affiliated with a Placed fiat. 

This would be actualized through a system for cultivating integers at scale. It is a pragmatic, bottom-up vision for terrestrial paradise. Pan-raiment is the end-state goal of the loose federation of interpersonal somatics labs. 

#### Expansive perspectives

Some enthusiasts propose that pan-raimentism should not just include human-to-human entanglements. They suggest that for a truly durable pan-raiment would require entangling with non-human persons, such as plant and animal friends.

Some researchers maintain that raiment can entangle not only with organic beings but also with ancestors and other non-corporeal sentiences — and that these entanglements provide an influx of nutrients that support humans’ core filaments especially during times of intense and complex life change, such as adolescence. 

Finally, a small but passionate group of universal pan-raimentists hold that every single thing has at least one tendre and thus the capacity to entangle. They posit that the pan-raiment should exclude nothing, not even, a cracked bell or a broken teapot. Their integers include numinous objects, such as the doorknobs and fire utensils of the Tales.
