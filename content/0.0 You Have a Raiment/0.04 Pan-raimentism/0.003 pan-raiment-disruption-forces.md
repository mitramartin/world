1. Dwindling supply of places that support the very basics of a healthy superskin and splay. 
2. Lack of access to interpersonal somatics practices to support raiment cultivation;
3. Cultural hyperfocus on optimizing currencies that do not align with raiment pluming, such as XP and Rotcoin;
4. Related to this, the fiat incarceration complex;
5. Absence of rituals to support developmental milestones in raiment maturation; and
6. Catastrophically monocultural governance systems that disregard the unique needs of our youngest citizens during the sensitive time when their raiment is growing.

We live in a world full of physical and temporal spaces that are rife with unhallowed technologies. Indoor-centricity, clock-centricity, and car-centricity are symptoms of nucleating cultural tendencies; all combine to contribute to a selficity and anti-conviviality that contributes to dysmorphiae and is thus disastrous for raiment health.
