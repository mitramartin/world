title: A note of acknowledgment
---
Pan-raimentism is at the very vanguard of raiment research. We would be amiss not to also acknowledge that this module is also most speculative, because these are questions and practices of entanglement at vast scales that are tough to imagine. Nobody has practical experience in any of this yet. 

Thinking on a whole-world level can bring up anti-raiment agents like cyncism, laziness, hopelessness, disaffection, and distrust. And those who theorize at that level may be a canvas for projections of other people’s fears, and thus deemed maniacally grandiose and conveniently othered in a variety of in other ways. 

We don’t mind that, and it also makes it quite handy that we’ve chosen (for now!) to remain anonymous authors.
