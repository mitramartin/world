In this next-to-last installment of our text inception you have a raiment, we consider the question of how cultures incentivize and amplify raiment excellence through reified, countable units, e.g., a currency. 

The pan-raimentists who specialized on slow learning observed that those settlements which were most well-woven shared two things: 

1. An orientation toward continuous and sprintful intrinsically-motivated learning, and 
2. Bountiful, precisely-worded, authentic gratefulness in multi directions

Now this mindframe is still atypical in Alexandria, where learning has been so totally colonized and even gratitude is often manipulative or worse, downright toxic. Those are such sad things. SlowXP in contrast provides a value-assignment system designed for the express purpose of focalizing people’s attention onto learning and generous true praise.
