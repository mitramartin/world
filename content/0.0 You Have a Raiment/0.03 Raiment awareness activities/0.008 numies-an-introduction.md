Numies are a technology of pheromonic, infrasonic, microtonal, polyrhythmic raiment-tonic sound parcels. They are cocreated by raiment-entwined artistic collaborators under beatific sponsorship.

The parcels are tightly folded, involuted hymns. The folding process is very laborious, and the techniques are beyond the scope of human fingers. Numies are hand-folded locally by indigenous deities through a process that can take anywhere from micro-moments to centuries. 

Once folded, a numie can stay implicate for aeons, waiting for the right raiment to activate it. When it is activated by a blessed collective, a numie will alchemically evolute and thence unfurl itself into human song perfectly suited to that person. Such a reaction is indeed alchemy and ramifies through several dimensions. 

### Examples
One wonderful example of two such artistic collaborators who discovered a high-flying tango together is actually from the vegetable kingdom. The Banisteriopsis caapi plant poet has collaborated closely with musical collaborator Psychotria viridis for centuries in service of human healing and evolution, resulting in a supernumie we now know as the tea ayahuasca.

Most numies yielded by human collaborations mimic features of the brew of this extraordinary partnership, e.g., unconditional love, exquisite embodiment, and mutual liberation in service and gentleness. The co-created poem book of Rumi and the Shams of Tabriz is another classic example.

A thrilling modern example of a numie is Torvill & Dean’s subsumement into The Boléro which is still blooming today. Many musical tango integers were able to formulate numies. Some examples are the works: Bahia Blanca; À Evaristo Carriego.

Within Meander's *Tales to be Told Aloud After Feasting Together*, we see the origami of many epic numies. The entire Kistler family is gifted with skills of numie-craftsmanship, whether through Arthur and his partner Dante’s *Bells! Bells!* musical, their children Penelope and Linus’ very tiny but perfect ringtone which formed the basis of an entirely new Kistler operating system, or even Cora Kistler’s early portrait work in collaboration with First Best & Only and later underworld works.
