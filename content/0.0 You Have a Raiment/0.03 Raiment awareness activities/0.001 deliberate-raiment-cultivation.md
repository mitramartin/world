Perhaps of late, since you entangled with this volume, you’ve been sensing the sweetness of your own growing tendres in small ways; maybe you’ve noticed those electric moments of tendre touch, or you’ve put in that extra effort to cloak. Possibly you’ve simply been a little more attentive to introductions, facing someone and pronouncing your noble name! We see you! 

Perhaps, you are therefore ready to learn about how to make the most of your extraordinary organ, your raiment. The material on Raiment awareness activities will walk you through all the specific, intentional ways you can join with others for the purposes of joyous raiment entanglement and [[0.002 benefits-of-interactive-somatics-to-the-raiment|all the wonders that proceed therefrom]]. There are many 100% non-blingy ways to participate in excitingly new raiment culture these days.

**Raiment-tonic practices**

Notwithstanding [[0.006 what-is-fone|Fone]] and all the challenges it brings, our era is actually quite a felicitous one when it comes to raiment wellbeing! This is in large part thanks to traditions of interactive somatics, organized by various interactive somatics guilds.

*Interactive somatics* practices provide a fertile ground for raiment health by creating a socially-reinforced methodology for refining critical [[0.010 superskin-skill-cloaking|superskin]] and [[0.012 splay-skill-entanglement|splay skills]]. These whole-bodied attention and improvisation practices challenge and refine the entire raiment through the way they invite attention and generosity at three scales simultaneously: the individual, the [[0.004 tanda-first-stage-of-entanglement|pair]], and the [[0.006 integer-third-stage-of-entanglement|small group]].

There are many, many different traditions of interpersonal somatics, each of which is enough to merit an entire text of its own. In our bioregion the most prominent and popular in this family of practices goes by the name **“tango”** (from “entangle” obviously).  This tradition usually happens in ritual spaces and takes shape according to musical containers created by divinely unhinged shamans.
