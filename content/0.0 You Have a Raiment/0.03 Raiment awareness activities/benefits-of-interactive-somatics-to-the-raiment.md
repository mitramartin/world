---
title: Benefits of interactive somatics to the raiment
---
Experienced interpersonal somatics practitioners can activate superstrong cloaking that’s light but also invincible and roomy enough for two; masters can even billow at will, making their superskin encompass many scores of people! Those who have been practicing for a decade or more have magnificent splays, with volumes of potent, extremely precise, wildly elastic tendres that can be finely chunked and orchestrated. And, is it a coincidence that these folks tend to plume earlier and more gorgeously, great rainbow gladiola spikes rocketing out of their shining nimbuses? 

Interactive somatics engender also a kind of euphoric, euphonic elation. The experience is heady and wholesome and has been likened to benevolent hallucinogenics, which motivates continued practice. Being part of such a field in any way, either actively or passively, is contagiously healthful for raiment.
