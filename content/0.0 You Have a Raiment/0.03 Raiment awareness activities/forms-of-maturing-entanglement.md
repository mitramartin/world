title: Forms of maturing entanglement
---
Entanglement begins with tendres sniffing one another, followed by an electrical touch. If the practitioners Face each other with a greeting that involves face, voice, and name in the sunshine, then a period of interlacing will normally ensue. Eventually the tendres unify into a single two-part functional unit. They are then entangled.  

Over time, entanglements naturally mature if the entangled persons continue to meet, walk, and exchange in sunlit settings. Interactive somatics guilds provide expert guidance to support safe, healthy maturation. Matured entanglements are incredibly beneficial, making each individual raiment orders-of-magnitude stronger.  
#### Tanda

For example, a tanda is the first stage of maturation. If two peoples’ tendres are regularly and daily interlaced for about week, this is a tanda. It is a simple way of activating congruence, discovering rhythm, and practicing tenderness that goes beyond simple affiliation. A tanda is up to 10x as strong as a solitary raiment, especially if it matures on or during a new moon or equinox event. 

A tanda can also easily dissolve after formation, through either member’s agency. Wearing clashing colors, evading eye-contact, or desynchronizing gestures are strategies to end an undesired tanda. 

#### Tandem

Multiple tandas with the same partner that take place across a variety of spaces and times naturally form a tandem. Tandems are on average about 100x as strong as a tandas, which is amazing, and they are difficult, maybe impossible, to disrupt, although they can go into hibernation for years. 

Because of the profound entanglement in a tandem (usually, the involved tendres actually become knitted into one another in intricately interconnected stretchful braids), each individual raiment benefits from a huge influx of complementary nutrients from the other, and they confer on one another a high degree of structural integrity. The unification endures across space and time, with tendre plaits continuing to send and receive information parcels and other minerals via breezes, fiats, or pollinating insects.   

Researchers who study raiment in cross-cultural settings have demonstrated that tandems occur even in cultures that deny raiment, and go by the term “marriage,” with a few important distinctions. 

- Tandems are non-excluding, but marriages often are exclusive
- Marriages seem to require mouth-to-mouth kissing, but tandems do not
- Tandems are completely non-bureaucratic, but marriages include extensive paperwork

In less marriage-centric cultures, tandems are also witnessed in the form of identity crushes, frenemies, work spouses, or any sort of “other significant other” arrangement. 
#### Integer

An integer is formed when five people’s tendres are fully interwoven. (So an integer requires ten tandems, using the combinations formula R =  [ N x (N-1) / 2 ], where N is the number of individuals.) And an integer is approximately 1000x as strong as a tandem raiment. 

The true cultivation of an integer is a multi-year project. Each individual configuration of dyads and triads within the integer develops a unique aspect of the maturing integer, and each individual sub-unit configuration requires several hundred hours worth of devoted attending via interpersonal somatic practice to mature.

Despite the effort required for integer cultivation, the benefits are quite worth the investment. Most experts now agree that there is a strong correlation between the number of integers in a community, and its safety, innovation, health outcomes, and resilience.
