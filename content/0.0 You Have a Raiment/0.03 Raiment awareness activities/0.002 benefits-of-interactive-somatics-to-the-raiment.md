Matured entanglements are incredibly beneficial, making each individual raiment orders-of-magnitude stronger.

And, is it a coincidence that these folks tend to [[0.015 raiment-pluming|plume]] earlier and more gorgeously, great rainbow gladiola spikes rocketing out of their shining nimbuses?

Being part of such a field in any way, either actively or passively, is contagiously healthful for raiment.
