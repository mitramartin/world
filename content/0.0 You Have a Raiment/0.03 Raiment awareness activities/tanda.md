title: Tanda
---
For example, a tanda is the first stage of entanglement maturation. If two peoples’ tendres are regularly and daily interlaced for about week, this is a tanda. It is a simple way of activating congruence, discovering rhythm, and practicing tenderness that goes beyond simple affiliation. A tanda is up to 10x as strong as a solitary raiment, especially if it matures on or during a new moon or equinox event. 

A tanda can also easily dissolve after formation, through either member’s agency. Wearing clashing colors, evading eye-contact, or desynchronizing gestures are strategies to end an undesired tanda.
