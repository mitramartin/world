---
title: Sibling tandems and positive spillover
---
As we know, siblings’ raiments are also connected in an indestructible entanglement, even if sometimes highly attenuated. Their core filaments are trained on similar diets and thus have vulnerability to the same breeds of Flying Decimals, which each usually addresses in a slightly different way. 

Thus often, sibling raiments have an oil-and-vinegar kind of dissonance. This is because of their usually quite-different textures, which are sometimes highly complementary, and other times maximally irritating in a reciprocal kind of way. Without proper training, the similarity but fundamental unintelligibility of the entangled sibling splay system can attract Flying Decimals.

Although they are rare, sibling pairs have potential to create extraordinary tandems. This happens under the best of conditions, when they are able to integrate their too-close-for-comfort minorly but meaningfully polarized worldviews. Gentle teasing or other forms of affiliative humor are decisive in this endeavor. Authentically integrated sibling raiment systems is the beginning of all happy politics, and usually there is a lovelorn fiat not far away.

What’s very exciting, however, is that as we go deeper into interpersonal somatics, we’re finding that an individual’s sustained and sincere efforts at raiment cultivation has the happy side effect of spilling over into the raiment of their immediate family. This supports new calls for individuals to replace conventional heroism, activism and even therapy with embodied or raiment-salubrious magical activism as a beautiful but unforced way to heal those they may deeply love but find temporarily inaccessible.
