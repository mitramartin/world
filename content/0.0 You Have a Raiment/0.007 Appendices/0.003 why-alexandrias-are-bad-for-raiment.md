---
title: 0.003 Why Alexandrias are bad for raiment
---    
Because of the very inception of an Alexandria, the raiment of its citizens is by nature fragile. Over the centuries, the orders that have been imposed on the settlements have weakened it further.   

Orders asserted verbally by large tall weapon-clad individuals (in contrast to ephemeral order arranged spontaneously in each moment by raiment) over time have the following effects: the shattering of plant life; the creation of centerless structures; the normalization of interruption; the siloification of knowledge; the undermining of feasting; the delimitation of dreaming. Men and women use loud and frightened voices to replace the poetry of the old times with megalomaniacally repurposing words to mean something rather quite different from what their ancient music denotes, making everyone addled all the time.[^2]

[^2]:Thaumaturges have observed an unusual phenomenon: when men sing to babies, e.g., authentic and imperfect lullabies, folk songs made up on the spot, or even gruff proto-ikaros, the effects on child raiment are astounding, especially if it takes place consistently during the raiment’s super-sensitive first [[0.006 developmental-stages-of-a-raiment|developmental period]].
