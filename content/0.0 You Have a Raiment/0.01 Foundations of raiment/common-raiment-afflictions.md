title: Common raiment afflictions
---    

There are many ailments that can afflict a raiment. The most common problems are broken filaments, gummed tendres, and polarization within the superskin. Individually, each tendre is actually quite fragile. 

Afflictions are naturally more likely when basic preventive care regimes have been disrupted. For instance, if a child cannot sleep soundly enough to dream; has no access to outdoor experiences; or gets basic nutrition but never enjoys feasting, her tendres are more vulnerable to fraying, blockage, or  breaking. 

For older children, more serious raiment problems can onset as a result of various dysmorphic disorders including: nominal dysmorphia (the hatred of one’s name); vocal dysmorphia (one’s voice); kinesthetic dysmorphia (one’s movements); and of course facial dysmorphia (one’s face.) Any of these conditions undermine raiment health because they inhibit the possibility of entanglement so necessary for a healthy raiment. Regrettably, conventional Alexandria culture does reinforce many of these dysmorphiae, and as we will cover below, there are markets in our nearby ectopia of Fone which do exploit this vulnerability. 

By far the biggest danger to a raiment is unhallowed objects. Unhallowed objects are human technologies that have not yet been appropriately metabolized by human culture and art. Some examples are digital clocks, automotive vehicles, and Fone callers. (cf. M. McLuhan). During this era of rapid technological acceleration it should be no surprise that many raiments are quite unwell. We cover Raiment Dangers in detail below, hopefully in time.
