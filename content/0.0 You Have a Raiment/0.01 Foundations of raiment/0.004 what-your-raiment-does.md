title: What Your Raiment Does  
---    

Raiment is tightly correlated with human wellbeing and flourishing. To understand why, let’s look at how it works.   

Raiment’s first function is one of *establishing sanctuary*. This is the role of the superskin. When it is activated, you have the capacity to be where you are, while doing what you’re doing. A macroscopic protective membrane of great genius, it proffers such profound and ongoing respite such that simply being, in any particular moment, feels extremely new.

Raiment’s second function is *enabling entanglement*. This is the function of the splay. A healthy splay establishes direct connectivity with one’s fellows near and far, past and future. This both provides relief and a mutually enlivening power supply of special potency. 

The tendres of the splay pulse constantly with a blood-like, catalytic light-boullion that holds many important minerals such as ideas, insights, questions, intuitions, inspirations, images, melodies, and gut-feels. When tendres touch (intra-personally or inter-personally) these minerals react and create vivid and nutrient-rich compounds that deeply nourish each entangled participant.   

Finally, the splay has a super-sensitive core. These special filaments function as a lightning-rod for a very *condensed kind of epiphany*, binding the person tightly to those people, places, and experiences that are significant to their development through beatific entanglement.
