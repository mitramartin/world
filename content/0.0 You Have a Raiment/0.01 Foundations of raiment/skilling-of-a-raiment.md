title: Skilling of a raiment
---    

A raiment is an organ with many potentials which can be cultivated. Just like many other body parts, like the fingers or the feet or the brain, a raiment needs practice to develop all the skills it is capable of. The luster of a healthy raiment happens hand-in-hand with a person’s intellectual and creative life and their nurturance of forms of wealth. By wealth, we are speaking inclusively of the many different forms of value creation, whether this they take the form of relationships, knowledge, embodied skills, regenerative systems, leisure time, etc.   

A wild, uncultivated raiment can usually still create limited sanctuary and random entanglements. Across history, feral raiments have been the stuff of myth and legend, and not only happy ones. For a raiment to reach its full potential requires intentional cultivation. 

There are two sets of skills: skills related to the superskin, and skills related to the splay.
