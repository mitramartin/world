Welcome. If you’re reading this, you are part of a secret society of people who either know, or believe, or want to believe, that raiment exists. You are among friends here. 

You won’t learn our names, however. Given how polarizing this topic has become, it’s not safe for us to share our identities; we must protect the [[0.005 who-can-see-raiment|seers]] among us. We pray the times will change. 

We can share that this handbook is collaboratively authored and curated by a collective of true raiment experts. We are framers, witches, ecologists, spiders, all with true seeing capabilities. We have been involved in the care, maintenance and healing of raiments since before the beginning of Alexandria. 

Let us begin. We intone aloud for you now: *You have a raiment*. It is so. The rest of this will help you discover all that it can do, and all that you can do with it. 
![[Element1.png]]
Essentially, this pamphlet covers the most critical aspects of being a person with a raiment during these times. We address [[0.003 anatomy-of-a-raiment|anatomy]], [[0.008 raiment-preventative-care|care]] and [[0.009 skilling-of-a-raiment|cultivation]], [[toxic-tools-and-noxious-ecologies|dangers]], and practices both [[raiment-tonic-practices|individual]] and [[contexts-for-deliberate-raiment-cultivation|collective]]. 

In addition to the main text, we will on varied unusual and revelatory artifacts, secretly vouchsafed to our Vestibule by various informants. At least one of them appears to have considerable access to [[welcome-to-loom-llc]|Loom LLC]’s inner workings including its new and very concerning raiment-processing factories. We are committed to preserving this individual’s total anonymity. 

Of course, raiment can’t be understood intellectually alone. This work is a companion to the  epic stories told in the Meander compendium *Tales to be Told Aloud after Feasting Together*, and in particular its title tale, *The Girl Who Got Stuck*. Here you have a front row seat, seeing the surgings and billowings, calamities and triumphs, of great raiments in action right here in our very own country.
