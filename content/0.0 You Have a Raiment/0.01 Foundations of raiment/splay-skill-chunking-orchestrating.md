#### Chunking

Chunking essentially refers to the skill of managing multiple simultaneous entanglements. This entails the capacity to consciously track how many active entanglements one has; and, to begin, pause, or close entanglements to ensure an optimal volume. 

It also involves learning to direct the same tendre or group of tendres toward the same companion repeatedly over time, so they can more easily acclimate to one another and discover their dance. 
#### Orchestrating

Tendre entanglements can be simple or complex. More complex entanglements means partnerships are forged across more tendres, or groups of tendres. And the inter-tendre connections themselves have richer patterning, e.g., multiple different simultaneous rhythms and intensities of approach/retreat and pausing.
### Pluming

A happy, healthy raiment under normal circumstances will blossom. A stalk will erupt from the center of the nimbus, grow, and blossom with a brightly colored richly ornamented flower spike: a Plume! At first these were seen as merely decorative but, as you will learn in future chapters, they actually have important personal and societal functions. In brief, plumes are used mostly in the context of attracting valuable and expansive inter-species partnerships.
