Finally it is time to unpack the fiat in its fullness. As we climb into this highly raiment-tonic topic, know that even if you’ve been brought up in the middlingest midtown of Alexandria your whole life, you have likely inhaled the good air emanating from a fiat at some point or other. You can benefit from the effervescence of a free fiat without knowing it, even if that fiat is several miles deep under the surface of the earth, frolicking in a subterranean basin or hide-and-seeking about an underground lough. 

But we all benefit from fiats even more immediately when we bring them to moonlight. May this module inform you on the etiquette you’d need for fiat charming, ultimately enabling you to apply their liquid genius to the raiment of you and your people.
