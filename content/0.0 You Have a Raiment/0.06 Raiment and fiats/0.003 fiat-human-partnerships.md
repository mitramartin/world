title: 0.003 Fiat-human partnerships
---
Fiats are important to understanding raiment because of the symbiotic relationship they have with human raiment. Naturally while the person dreams, a free fiat will groom them. It removes and eats raiment mites, untangles tendres, and shines the nimbus. Foraging for raiment mites is a nightly activity. 

Towards the end of their Pouring phase, fiats become irresistibly attracted to human raiment plumes. As you probably remember, only very healthy raiments erupt [[0.015 raiment-pluming|plumes]]. During this sensitive time, conjugal partnerships can occur between fiat, human, and place. 

It begins when a plumed human chooses her fiat. She then goes to a Place she loves during optimal moonlight, beckons her fiat there, and tickles it with her raiment plume. If the fiat consents to the partnership, it immediately Pools which means it has committed to the unique combination of that human and that place. The human (regardless of its gender) becomes the fiat’s King. A well-cared-for fiat then protects the raiment of the king and all of the king’s affiliates/friends/cousins as a kind of external immune system for the rest of its life, or as long as the king is nice to it (brushes it, talks to it, tickles it, etc.). The fiat aerosolizes various compounds that are advantageous to raiment flora, and which encourage entanglement of human tendres. As a result, settlements affiliated with Placed fiats tend to create more tandems, integers, and thus numies. 

Occasionally the fiat may mysteriously annul the partnership; nobody knows why. It is perhaps this unpredictability that has led to very [[0.004 different-cultural-interpretations-of-the-fiat|different cultural interpretations of the fiat]].

Of course, not everyone is skilled at fiat handling (although everyone can learn and there are more than enough fiats). Certain humans are fiat-whisperers and have a natural knack for fiat-positive relationships. Humans who are not as interested in this can get by and even flourish without their own fiat, e.g., as part of an [[0.006 integer-third-stage-of-entanglement|Integer]] with a fiat-handler
