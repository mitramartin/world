title: 0.006 A raiment-conserving currency
---
The [[0.001 introduction-to-pan-raimentism|pan-raimentists]] who specialized on slow learning observed that those settlements which were most well-woven shared two things: 

1. An orientation toward continuous and sprintful intrinsically-motivated learning, and 
2. Bountiful, precisely-worded, authentic gratefulness in multi directions

Now this mindframe is still atypical in Alexandria, where learning has been so totally colonized and even gratitude is often manipulative or worse, downright toxic. Those are such sad things. [[notes-for-slow-XP-system-1|SlowXP]] in contrast provides a value-assignment system designed for the express purpose of focalizing people’s attention onto learning and [[gratefulness-day|generous true praise]].

The architects of SlowXP believed that a system built to actually center learning would be playful, a little unpredictable, would take away the emphasis on winners and losers, and would encourage people to focus on the long game and thus achieve pan-raiment. It was to be more like a game with elements of randomness and madhatterdom; less like serious, heavy schoolish undertaking. People would be incentivized to put in the highest quality energy and attention all the time, since that is the only possible way to win in the long run.

Investing in a SlowXP ecosystem has turned out to be [[0.002 benefits-of-interactive-somatics-to-the-raiment||gorgeously healthful for ones’ raiment]] not only because it is way fun; it also helps people attend to and amplify the things that support entanglement. There is a correlation between those who render SlowXP in the greatest amounts, and those who have the grandest, most cerebral and colorful plumes. So far, SlowXP is the only currency system that seems to have any kind of beneficial effect on raiment whatsoever, which is very important for would-be [[0.003 fiat-human-partnerships|fiat-bewitchers]].
