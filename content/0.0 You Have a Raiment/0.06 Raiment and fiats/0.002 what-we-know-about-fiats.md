title: 0.002 What we know about fiats
---
We cannot lie: fiats are a mysterious species and still a subject of study for the Raiment Wellness Collective. Here is what we know about their origins, bodies, life cycles, and diet. 
### Where fiats come from

There is a deep underground fiat reservoir, like a water table. When the taux circle and sing, occasionally the result is that a baby fiat is hatched/plucked/coalesced. 
### The body of the fiat

Since time immemorial, fiats’ bodies have been the lifewater of civilization. Fiats have liquid bodies which can move quickly and do limited shapeshifting, like water nymphs. In terms of their life cycles, they go through a metamorphosis phase, like caterpillars.
* During their first phase of life, their ***Pouring***, they are in active flux across their terrain. 
* During their second phase of life, their ***Placing***, they pool under a specific earthly location.  
### Diet of the fiat

Fiats have a diverse diet, including minerals from the salt of underground catacomb walls; mites from human raiment; moonlight; and luminosity from celestial numies.
