title: 0.002 Interpersonal somatics modality of choice
---
The prevailing interpersonal somatics system used these days in Meander for fostering well-woven and highly refined raiment is [[immutable-laws-of-tango-os|tango]]. Doubtless there are other marvelous methodologies; we enthusiastically refer you to your local seers for guidance. 

But as tango is our regional mode, and quite a syncretic one at that, we shall double-double down on it in this text, because, as we all know it takes two! The ensuing unit thus attempts to compress the five-dimensional[^1] reality of tango into a linear sequence of words. 

Essentially, this will translate these principles of paradise into more of a download whereby the [[0.002 the proposal of a pan-raiment|garden of pan-raiment]] is enacted. We focus specifically on how the tango body and ritual space support the ongoing upgrade.
