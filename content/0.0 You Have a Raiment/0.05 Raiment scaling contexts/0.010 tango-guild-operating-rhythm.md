title: 0.010 Tango Guild operating rhythm
---
The guild sponsors three contexts where raiment fitness activities take shape in a regular rhythm.

1. **Práctica.** Daily practicas are for practicing micro missions, solo and in pairs. There is a basic core curriculum that peers help each other learn via structured social learning fellowships. It includes concepts like yearning; mirroring; looking for, listening to, and seeing each other; walking together; pausing together; interactive grammar basics; resolving dissonance of various kinds; and the study of tango planes (folding/unfolding).

2. **Festival.** Festival happens weekly at least. Here is where members gather for convivial culinary practice and the exchange of thought leadership content developed and investigated in Práctica. Festivals are often visibly or invisibly anchored by the presence of Tango Guild deities and their musical apprentices. The event begins with co-creative feasting; proceeds with members sharing elements of their works-in-process; and culminates in total-involvement all-including whirl of powerfully numinous dancing. 

3. **Cypher.** Cypher is an approximately annual focalization that usually aligns with an auspicious celestial event (a solstice, an equinox, an eclipse). It goes on for several evenings and includes camping/slumber-partying. Cypher is a showcase and celebration of the intensive efforts of tandems and integers. These are explicated through curated contemplative improvisational games designed to create palpable radiance and encourage numie fruiting. Also, a special curated space is held for offerings of all those turning Sixteen that year officially welcoming them as fully-franchised adult community members. Anyways, it is something like a fringe festival, something like a rap battle, something like rain dance. Cypher time is super auspicious and accelerates R&D efforts leading up to it. Posters are painted, capstones are culminated, SlowXP posters are unveiled.

Together, these three contexts reinforce the effective [[0.005 tango-enabled-raiment-research-domains|ecology of practices]] that underpins the successful establishment of [[0.004 tanda-first-stage-of-entanglement|tandas]], [[0.005 tandem-second-stage-of-entanglement|tandems]], and [[0.006 integer-third-stage-of-entanglement|integers]], and this is why where you find [[0.009 the-tango-guild|Tango Guilds]], you find [[0.008 numies-an-introduction|numies]] of all kinds.
