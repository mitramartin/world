Now that you are aware of the larger [[0.002 the proposal of a pan-raiment|pan-raiment vision]] and the [[0.005 pan-raiment-resource requirements|difficult sales, marketing, and intercultural relations work]] of building it, let’s return to the crucial challenge of any individual’s [[0.001 deliberate-raiment-cultivation|raiment cultivation]] in these times. Because, there will only be a pan-raiment to the extent that any single person is supported in deliberately cultivating their own organ of sanctuary and interconnectedness. 

Perhaps you’ve noticed: Even though it’s great to dance a [[0.004 tanda-first-stage-of-entanglement|tanda]], be in [[0.005 tandem-second-stage-of-entanglement|tandem]], or have an [[0.006 integer-third-stage-of-entanglement|integer]] to call on in times of need, and even though everyone would love to have multi-[[0.008 numies-an-introduction|numies]] floating off of them every day like a loveliness of ladybugs, developing even any single one of these things ***takes a lot of hard work***. 

Most communities therefore prioritize [[0.002 interpersonal-somatics-modality-of-choice|electing a specific interpersonal somatics modality]] and attending [[0.009 the-tango-guild|context]] which sponsors and incentivizes raiment fitness activities, making it easier for inhabitants to regularly choose to practice, investigate, and invest in the difficult and fraught project of Flying Decimal-evasion and raiment-building.
