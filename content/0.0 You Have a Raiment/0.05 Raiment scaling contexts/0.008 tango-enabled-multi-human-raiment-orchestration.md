title: 0.008 Multi-human raiment orchestration
---
Tango operates on the level of the Integer to undo the typical patternings that are exhibited in most congregations of thinking-centric humans. It helps the user identify and change themes like the following: 

- **Coercion, imposing one’s will on others.** This is changed to following the follower. 
- **Neglect of structured inquiry/lack of curiosity.** This orientation is replaced with a practice of interrogating one’s world. 
- **Fear and rejection of anything woo-woo.** This turns into a faith practice of choice. 
- **The expectation that others will read your mind.** Instead, practitioners adopt healthy and persistent self-expression. 
- **Disheartenment from people not responding as you wish.** This is transformed to ingenuity in iterative gambit creation.
- **Distraction, plagiarizing, and avoiding.** These are replaced by prioritizing imagination and actively making space for it.  

At this level, these orchestrations literally de-fuse the atomic blame bombs that cause Flying Decimals to surge, such as fury, nihilism, hardness, dread, depression, terror, etc. which of course means that this work is directly related to the circuitry of not only the splay but the most core central filaments at the heart of one’s raiment — e.g., those connected to ones’ ancestral partners.
