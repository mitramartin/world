title: 0.004 Tango Guild access points
---
Although it is not technically a mystery school, the Tango Church provides no overt gateway to its practice community, including the ones provided here. Here are the ways that members have found it 1) By passing through the portal at the end of the Algorithm Tundra (at the bottom of Fone) 2) Getting there via swimming directly into the [[fones-famous-cracks|Crack in Fone]] 3) Simply by meandering — walking the youth/death campus without objective or plan.

Fone-based entries take the visitor to the Guild via an irreal inverse mirror landscape, appropriately called [[enof-welcome-center-signage|ENOF]], which is usually available to those who have had enough of Fone. ENOF is peopled with [[0.003 raiment-supporting-deities|sponsoring deities]] that help humans acclimate to the very different weather that the Guild works hard to create to support the practice.
