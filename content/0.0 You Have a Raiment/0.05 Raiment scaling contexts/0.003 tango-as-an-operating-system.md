title: 0.003 Tango as an operating system
---
Tango is an operating system. We want to be extremely specific here so you understand what we mean and exactly how and why tango, as an operating system, is well-suited to the zeitgeist.   

An operating system is the interface, or lens, between a physically real thing and a set of possible options or behaviors. 

Our usual, conventional operating system for humans is so entrenched as to go basically unnoticed by everyone, like the water the fish swim in. This operating system is called “thinking.” Throughout Alexandria it provides the main go-to interface between bodies and possibilities. 

What we now know is that, as an operating system, “thinking” is rather obtuse and anemic. Mainly, because of its limited inputs and limited outputs. For one thing, it isn’t able to access raiment-blood, or anything that pulses through raiment, as an input. For another thing, it is only able to output in the form of words and sentences. A lot of the outputs of mere thinking are as a result rather flat-footed, or ham-handed, or just lackluster.  
  
Tangoing, on the other hand, is [[immutable-laws-of-tango-os|an operating system which was lovingly hand-coded by raiment-seers in Argentina]] around a hundred years ago, plus or minus. Therefore it has the capability to accept many more kinds of inputs, not only from one’s own raiment but also from that of others. As a raiment-native system, its outputs are also more multidimensional (see above discussion on numies.) The tango has many regional manifestations (West Coast tango, sockfoot tango, metta tango, sun tango, ntango[^1], _tango, xango, and many many others) but they all form part of the same underlying kernel of unconditional all-inclusive embodied finely-discerning love.
