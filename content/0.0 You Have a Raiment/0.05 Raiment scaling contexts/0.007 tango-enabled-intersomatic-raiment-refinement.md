title: 0.007 Intersomatic raiment refinement
---
Tango works with habitual programming relating to a person’s relationships, via tandas and tandems, refactoring those operations that undermine healthy entangling and beyond. The main themes here are:  

- **Obliviousness to sensory perception.** One learns to enjoy respectful interactive bodily sensuality
- **Unconsciousness of another human body in space.** One develops capacity to notice and embrace partners in detail
- **Insensible to partner’s ideas.** One discovers multimedia listening at fractal scales 
- **Unrelenting and random content creation.** One discovers how to match one’s own content creation to one’s context
- **Weakness and timidity.** One learns to decisively choose power
- **Indirect, oblique, shy about sharing micro-content or soliciting it.** One learns unapologetic directness
- **Mushy or uncertain boundaries.** One learns to choose her distance and refine it ongoingly
    
Many sustained and diverse entanglements provide the basis and toolset for engaging these themes, through which the splay and the tendres become excitingly voluminous, like a Gyphy Caravan conditioner commercial, except actually for real! This also destroys Decimals that specialize on shame, self-absorption, worry, impatience, sadness, anger, and victimhood, simultaneously making the splay into a definite anti-Detrivore force-field.
