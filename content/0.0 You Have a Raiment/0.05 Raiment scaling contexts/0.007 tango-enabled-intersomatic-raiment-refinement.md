### Intersomatic Raiment Refinements

The tango works with programs relating to a person’s relationships, via tandas and tandems, refactoring those operations that undermine healthy entangling and beyond. There are seven operant themes here: ***Obliviousness, unconsciousness, insensibility, randomness, timidity, obliqueness, and mushy boundaries.***   

Sustained and diverse entanglements provide the basis and toolset for engaging these themes, through which the splay and the tendres become excitingly voluminous, like a Gyphy Caravan conditioner commercial, except actually for real! The practice also addles Decimals that specialize on shame, self-absorption, worry, impatience, sadness, anger, and victimhood, simultaneously making the splay into an anti-Detrivore force-field.
