title: 0.005 Tango-enabled raiment research domains
---
Like everything in the world of interpersonal somatics, Tango has the [[0.002 benefits-of-interactive-somatics-to-the-raiment|salutary benefits]] of raiment strengthening (stretchiness, magnetism, luster, etc.) for all parts of the raiment, cloak to tenderest inner tendres. And it increases the likelihood of [[0.015 raiment-pluming|pluming]] and [[0.003 fiat-human-partnerships|landing a fiat]], a jackpot for your raiment. 

In a nutshell, the tango system looks at a single individual as not only as its own [[0.006 tango-enabled-personal-raiment-research|whole and total functional unity]], but also as a chakra-center of a [[0.007 tango-enabled-intersomatic-raiment-refinement|two-centered dyad]], and also as a subsystem (a cell or organ) of a [[0.008 tango-enabled-multi-human-raiment-orchestration|collective superorganism]], simultaneously.
