---
title: Contexts for deliberate raiment cultivation
---
Now that you are aware of the larger pan-raiment vision and the difficult sales, marketing, and intercultural relations work of building it, let’s return to the crucial challenge of any individual’s raiment cultivation in these times. Because there will only be a pan-raiment to the extent that any single person is supported in deliberately cultivating their own organ of sanctuary and interconnectedness. 

Perhaps you’ve noticed: Even though it’s great to dance a tanda, be in tandem, or have an integer to call on in times of need, and even though everyone would love to have multi-numies floating off of them every day like a loveliness of ladybugs, developing even any single one of these things takes a lot of hard work. 

Most communities therefore prioritize creating a solid context which sponsors and incentivizes raiment fitness activities, making it easier for inhabitants to regularly choose to practice, investigate, and invest in the difficult and fraught project of Flying Decimal-evasion and raiment-building. 

The prevailing interpersonal somatics system used these days in Meander for fostering well-woven and highly refined raiment is tango. As systems go, it is quite a syncretic one.
