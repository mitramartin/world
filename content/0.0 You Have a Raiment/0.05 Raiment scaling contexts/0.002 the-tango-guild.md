---
title: 0.002 The Tango Guild
---
The Tango Guild is a geographically unbundled city-state situated all through and around and across the various Alexandrias. The purpose of the Guild is to create motivating contexts for structured raiment fitness practices.

The Tango Guild describes itself as an “ultra-denominational center for numinous interpersonal somatics.” Since this language can be opaque and/or off-putting, we will unpack the nature of this important institution using more accessible terms. 

Many members of the tango guild don’t like the appellation “guild,” noting correctly that it has not been accredited as a guild by Alexandria’s Guild Accreditation Council (led by J. Antar). And they also don’t so much like the idea of guilds and the values they implicate. Others say that “guild” is accurate regardless of the Alexandria’s Guild Accreditation Council and call it that anyway. 

Think of the fellowship as a dedicated community of practice, focused on motivating raiment-strengthening activities on multiple scales simultaneously.
