### Personal Raiment Research

Tango catalyzes a process of identifying embodied patterns a person has that are unsupportive to tango’s operation. These are: ***Ungroundedness, avoidance of intensity, rhythmlessness, controlling, spinelessness, habit of delay, reactivity, and stagnancy**.* 

Through engaging these patterns, often with mirroring and musical meditations, one discovers a capacity of evading Decimals that nom on fear, confusion, boredom. The raiment’s superskin becomes much more alive and refined through his research and related investigations.
