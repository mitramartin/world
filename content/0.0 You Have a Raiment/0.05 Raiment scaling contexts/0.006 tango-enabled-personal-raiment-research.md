title: 0.006 Personal raiment research
---
The tango catalyzes a process of identifying embodied patterns a person has that are unsupportive to tango’s operation. During this time one is engaged with the following eight themes, usually through musical meditations and missions that involve mirrors of many kinds.    

- Ungroundedness. The goal is to become more aware of contact of feet on the earth
- Avoidance of intensity. The aim is to more able to open to extremes
- Chaos or rhythmlessness. The direction is toward being more able to notice cycles at many scales
- Controlling everything. We look for increasing the possibility of fluency 
- Spinelessness. We cultivate becoming able to stand upright in radiant alignment
- Fear of taking a step. Instead, we learn to extend the body and step boldly
- Reacting compulsively to stimulus. Eventually we are able to organize around phrases and the deadlines they incorporate
- Being stubborn, stuck, or stagnant. We practice pivoting with precision to change direction exactly as much as needed
  
A lot of the information for engaging these themes is in the raiment’s superskin, which becomes much more alive and refined through his research and related investigations. Also, it decimates those lame Decimals that like to nom on fear, confusion, boredom.
