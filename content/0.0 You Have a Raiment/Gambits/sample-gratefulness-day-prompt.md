---
title: Sample Gratefulness Day prompt
---
Who is someone you are thankful for but you never told them? It could be someone who’s real and alive or not. 

Who is someone that has amazing qualities that you would like to praise? 

Who is someone who has helped you learn? Maybe they helped you learn how to do something, or you learned just by being around them. Or maybe they helped you learn by clearly showing you a part of yourself you don’t like very much, helping you understand it better.

You can send them SlowXP now. If you want, write a thank you letter telling them what you notice about them and how they have affected you.
