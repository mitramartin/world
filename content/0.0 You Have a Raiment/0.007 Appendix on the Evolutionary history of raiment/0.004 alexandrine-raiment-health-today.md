title: 0.004 Alexandrine raiment health today
---    
Over the past decade the state of Alexandrine raiment health has worsened about fivefold. This has happened concurrently with the rise and market dominance of what was a previously little-known centuries-old ad-driven media company, [[welcome-to-loom-llc|Loom LLC, now stepping into the limelight of the unicorn edtech world.]] Loom LLC had always capitalized on making good business out of bad news, and had no qualms about using hellish or twisted content to meet monthly targets. 

It was when Loom cracked the nanotechnology of smart holes to Inferno (eventually rebranded Fone Holes) that the business model really clicked into place. It took them years of experimenting,[^1] but eventually it happened: cheap, universally accessible Fone tourism was suddenly possible, so easy to use that even the young could go. Businesswise, it was an innovative move to establish Fone as the nexus of education for every child across all the Alexandrias. Raimentwise, it was catastrophic.

[^1]: Most experimentation happened in the fast-ed XP Arcade franchise the company established as a free alternative to conventional schooling. Through the network of XP Arcades, they were able to quickly develop a user base, explore incentive patterns, develop a proprietary experience points currency (Hearts), and discover a range of profitable ad products that would perform well in an educational context. The bricks-and-mortar arcades supported experimentation through side revenue streams like food service.
