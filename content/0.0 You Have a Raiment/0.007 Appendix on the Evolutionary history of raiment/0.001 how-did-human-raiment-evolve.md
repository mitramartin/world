---
title: 0.001 How did human raiment evolve?   
---    
Raiment evolved to support the emergent human need for the sublime, improvisatory orchestration of multi- and trans-personal units. The first raiment nubs are visible in archaeological record of our ancestors who practiced alloparenting. Apparently, cooperative, collaborative childrearing called forth the need for a kind of spontaneous, ongoing, criss-crossing, inter-generational weaving of human energies which was not possible using only previously available sensory organs. Cultures with rich traditions involving communal music-making and interactive somatic arts show accelerated raiment unfoldment.
