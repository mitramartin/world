title: 0.005 Alexandria through a seer’s eyes
---    
For those practiced in seeing, even the briefest of errands in Alexandria are painful. 

Amputated tendres rotting in piles in the corners of bus shelters, buzzed over by foaming Decimal larvae. 

Blighted bits of nimbus cracked and strewn like brittle coral. 

Dirty angel-hair filaments clogging the sewer grates, gasping like jellyfish clinging to bits of life, but nibbled savagely by animals that look like neither rat nor dog nor hedgehog. 

You cover your nose and mouth with a flowered handkerchief. You zip your superskin tightly around you. You stay vigilant for the seduction of the pockmarked Fone holes. You hurry on to meet your informant. 

(In such a landscape, it’s very unusual to encounter someone with even a chunk of functioning filaments, such as Penelope Kistler.)
