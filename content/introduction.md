# Introduction

Welcome. If you’re reading this, you are part of a secret society of people who either know, or believe, or want to believe, that raiment exists. You are among friends here. 

You won’t learn our names, however. Given how polarizing this topic has become, it’s not safe for us to share our identities; we must protect the seers among us. We pray the times will change. 

We can share that this handbook is collaboratively authored and curated by a collective of true raiment experts. We are framers, witches, ecologists, spiders, all with seeing capabilities. We have been involved in the care, maintenance and healing of raiments since before the beginning of Alexandria. 

Let us begin. We intone aloud for you now: *You have a raiment*. It is so. The rest of this will help you discover all that it can do, and all that you can do with it. 

This text covers aspects of being a person with a raiment during these times. We will address anatomy, care and cultivation, dangers, and practices both individual and collective. 

We draw on varied unusual and revelatory artifacts, secretly vouchsafed to our Vestibule by various informants. At least one of them appears to have considerable access to Loom LLC’s inner workings including its new and very concerning raiment-processing factories. We are committed to preserving this individual’s total anonymity. 

Of course, raiment can’t be understood intellectually alone. This work also includes epic stories, in which you will have a front row seat, seeing the surgings and billowings, calamities and triumphs, of great raiments in action right here in our very own country. 
  
We cannot promise these tales to be brightly-lit and chipper. In fact, as part of your onboarding, we must first require that you first acquaint yourself with one of the many accounts of recent raiment ravishing: the story of Meander’s first child.
