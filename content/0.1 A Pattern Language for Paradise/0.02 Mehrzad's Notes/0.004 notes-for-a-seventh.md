The child has teeth  

A child with teeth = a predator

They now have the power to use their mouth to destroy and create

Purpose of the Seventh is to invite the child to direct her prodigious powers toward prosocial purposes  

The locus of festivity: the power of the child’s voice. She sings, she chooses, she thanks

Earning new privileges (joining Parliament), new peers (an Integer), new realms (Fone) (!) 

Recipe
- The child selects, prepares, presents a song to thank their polyparents
- The child introspects (Walkabout)
- The child publicly announces the governance circles they want to join, is welcomed 
- The child receives Fone caller, unboxes publicly, receives their Integer
