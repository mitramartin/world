The conventional currency of learning is defunct. Here is a new approach. 

“Currency of learning” = all the markers that educators use, in order to help their customers (e.g., students/their families/their employers) feel confident that learning has happened. Things like grades, transcripts, certificates, degrees, symbols like “stars” or badges, and so forth.

Why the conventional system is defunct: Metrics like grades, scores, GPAs, XP of all kinds, are too blunt and conservative of an instrument to measure learning. Often inaccurate. 

- Don’t capture relationships. But most learning happens through relationships.  
- People can and often do score high without developing social/emotional skills. But social and emotional skills are the most important skills for our culture right now. 
- Over-relies on institutional prestige brands, which don’t have much incentive to innovate with the times — and often exclude groups of learners. 
- Doesn’t support zero-to-five year olds’ learning. They are our most important citizens, their brains are developing at the fastest rate.

How people learn 

- By doing projects
- By immersing in a community
- By developing a craft with lots of practice and feedback
- By developing relationships with mentors, mentees, collaborators, friends
- By helping others learn

Proposal: What if we could create a currency system that mirrors how learning actually works?
