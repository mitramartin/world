A young child’s learning takes place within the extended family and community. The way the Slow XP system works for children is that it formally invites a collective of selected polyparents to participate in the young child’s learning and development. And it incentivizes them to act with honor on the child’s behalf. Also, it encourages the community to collaborate to ensure the child’s learning is well-rounded and covers the major key developmentally appropriate areas. 

- The parents of the child can register up to 12 polyparents when a woman is pregnant. 
- As they witness one another helping the child learn, polyparents can give points to other polyparents, in the child’s name. The points are recorded as if the child granted them; the name of the polyparent who initiated the transfer is not recorded and irretrievable. (Primary parents don’t give each other points.) 
- When points are given, the giver has to choose which category they relate to. There are seven macro categories of polyparenting: Story, Nature, Práctical Life, Music, Play, Contemplative, Social & Emotional. 
- The random multiplier, and random consumption of SlowXP, do not apply to this population. 
- The child will retain access to the full registry of all the SlowXP that were made in their name, and who the points gifts went to, throughout their lifetime. 

On the child’s 7th birthday, the points are extracted into an NFT art piece that memorializes the efforts of the polyparents, and expresses the degree of well-roundedness of the polyparents' activities across the seven categories. 

Friction between members of polyparent collectives is not unheard of. Conflict-softening and restorative practices are required for an endeavor like this.
