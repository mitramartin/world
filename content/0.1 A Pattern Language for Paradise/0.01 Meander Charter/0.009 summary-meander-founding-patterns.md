Thanks for reading this pamphlet covering some of the patterns we have made explicit as part of the founding of Meander. We hope it gives you a sense of the world-building we are attempting. Maybe it will also spark ideas for pan-raiment-conducive projects you might wish to incubate. 

Hopefully you also get some sense of how hard and humbling it is to attempt to live these out in actual reality. Because you can never really escape the monsters all around you and inside you, the Flying Decimals surges, and the temptations of your own Fone caller. 

Even one single lost or confused person can obliterate the hope for paradise. No child is safe until all of them are. So we can’t forget about anybody while we are building a shining new world. We have to remember them, reach toward them, entangle them — even though the hard hard work of building this new world might seem like it takes up all we have. Big new geopolitical paradise patterns might help, over time, if they are introduced with love. But the real work happens through your raiment, through the healing, anti-acidic, cross-cutting tandas you reach tenderly toward with your true arts. 

In lovingkindness,   

Sousana and Mehrzad
