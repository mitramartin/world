---
title: Theses of onboarding
source: Meander General Circle archives
---
Onboarding is the pattern of training unhallowed devices so they can be in the service of raiment flourishing, whereby we assert alertness in the face of new things we don’t know much about. 

It is leaning into dialogue when we put a new invention in front of a child, or anyone; it is about providing just supports when we invite them into a new space, a new culture. 

We are all trying to metabolize the new — it is complicated, often indigestible, sometimes hot, sometimes acid, sometimes numbing. 

Those who have the capacity to deal with it earliest have a responsibility to help others safely on board. These tend to be people who hold an embodied form of sanity. 

Proposal: When new devices enter the noosphere, they are deployed first to artists and seers to metabolize.
