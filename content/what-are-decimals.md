# What are Decimals? 

Decimals, a three-cornered bat-like vampiric parasite, are a nuisance to human raiment. They latch onto the ends of tendres, sucking out flora to feed on, and pumping them full of sticky liquid excrement. 

Normally, Decimal populations are held in check by their natural predators, the Fone spiders. However, recently, Decimal numbers have surged. Several experts have suggested that this is because the automations that Loom LLC has grafted onto certain species of flying Decimals make them more difficult for spiders to catch.
