title: Catalog of Polyparenting Frictions
author: Teryn Bareilles
---
There are lots of areas I wish I’d thought more about before starting this damn cacophony. It is stunning how differently our gaggle of hand-selected polyparents think about these things! We should deliberate and document smart defaults related to these areas to smooth things out for future groups. Specifically, how to navigate different behaviors and mindsets among polyparents relating to:

- Animals - killing spiders, killing rodents, fishing, eating meat
- Prayers/Saying grace - when, how often, how long, talking about/to God
- Being on time - navigating different cultural expectations, e.g. for start of meal
- Kissing - needing explicit consent or not
- Tickling the child - yes or no
- Adult nudity - yes or no, how much, when, and when not
- Praise - kinds of evaluative language and if it is called for/under what circumstances 
- Diet - drawing the line between intelligent moderation and permissiveness 
- Overprogramming - difficulties choosing between different polyparents’ planned activities
- Tools - on the use of anything unhalllowed including of course Fone (algorithms, power tools, things spiked/bladelike); onboarding requirements
- Security - protocols for limiting access to spaces that contain difficult tools (above)   
- Cleanliness - being explicit about what it looks like
- Talking about difficult matters - helping the child see the world through the lens of peace + responsibility/accountability, not vilifying others

To do: Build a handbook for future collectives along with a more structured set of conflict-softening and restorative practice
