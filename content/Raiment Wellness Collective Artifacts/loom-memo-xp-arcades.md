*Subject: Exciting News | Future of Education* 

On behalf of the Loom Future Of Education Council, I am very happy to report that effective Monday morning we will provide all children under eighteen living in Alexandria free access to Loom’s expanding network of XP Arcades. 

We believe that the XP Arcade represents the future of education in its most perfect form. Each child is provided with an individualized, fully gamified curriculum curated by state-of-the-art algorithms. Progress can be tracked and data analytics provided in a variety of views, so you can always have confidence that your child is learning. Built-in leaderboards ensure continuous engagement and spark meaningful motivation. And, gathering at XP Arcades foster all-important socialization skills. 

Related to this I’m happy to announce a new acquisition. Moving forward Loom will be the owner of the regional Pizza Barn franchise, a bricks-and-mortar network which will house new XP Arcades. This partnership will provide much-needed cafeteria services for students, 24-hours. The menu includes many healthy options including vegetable-only pizzas, gluten-free breadsticks, and fruit cups. Meal cards are available at the company store for a discount. 

We know many of you are eager to receive Fone Callers for your children, so that their education can continue at all times, and our teams are working around the clock to make this reality available for every family! In the meantime, Ivy Guild is making available another 2000 donated Callers in its upcoming lottery, so do place a Brick. 

We all know and are proud of our community’s track record of extraordinary academic achievement. Many Alexandrians are trailblazers and pioneers, billionaires and geniuses, peerless in all they do. We are glad to be able to continue this legacy in a manner that is a better fit with the societal conditions we now find ourselves in. We welcome your feedback via the engagement survey that you’ll find in your Vestibule next Monday. Thank you and if you have any questions please do place a Brick in our public vestibule.

Primum, optimum et unicum!

-Jannah
