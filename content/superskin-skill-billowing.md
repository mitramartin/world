#### Billowing

Can you remember a time when you were entranced into a timeless flow state by the presence of another person? If so, you have probably been in their billowing. 

When a person becomes very practiced at [[superskin-skill-cloaking|cloaking]], over time their light but strong filaments may actually grow in dimension. Slowly, with practice, they can build the capacity to include other people within the scope of their cloak. Thus their body creates a contagious attentive attuned space. It’s experienced as a soft and contentless charisma. What’s actually happening on a molecular level is a simple billowing. Mystics, singers, and improvisers are professional billowers. They are adept at alchemizing intensely concentrated individual emotion into a collective protective force-field.
