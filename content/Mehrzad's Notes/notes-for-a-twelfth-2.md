Purpose: 
Core idea is to free the child to ask questions that will open her life  

Asking a question as a radical & revolutionary act

Asking a question = commanding the Universe, commanding and expecting an answer

Asking a question = humility, making a promise, making a commitment to integrate the answer

Why children don’t ask questions? 

- Asked a question in the past that embarrassed someone
- Can’t think of a question
- Have too many questions — can’t choose
- Too busy to be curious, no time to think of questions to ask
- Don’t know they are “allowed” to ask questions
- They have questions but don’t have opportunities to ask them
- Don’t know how to construct a question, don’t know what kinds of questions to ask

The cycle of questions: 1) wondering 2) crafting 3) courage: asking! 4) mulling  

Questions can be generated from material exposed by the Epic

Invited guests to help the child formulate the question by

- offering examples
- dialogue  

The youth is taught how to reach the oracle

The youth must Ask alone
