---
title: Notes on the children’s parliament
---

Conventional assumption: *children cannot govern*

As a result: they grow up without practicing governance

If childhood proceeds without child-led governments, society will continue to be led by adults who have never learned the skills of governance. 

If government proceeds without childrens’ voices, society will never properly accommodate childrens’ bodies. 

Qualities children bring to politics: imagination, sense of fairness, pragmatism

How can we let children practice making decisions together / and feel the implications of their decisions? 

Children’s Parliaments concept
  
Helping children practice governance: things we can do: 

- Define domains in which they can make simple decisions w/o adult involvement
- Model consent-based decision-making and help them see why it’s important
- Help them create circles that are appropriately sized so all voices can be heard
- Define roles that they can fulfill, appropriate to their age (e.g., leader, note taker, time keeper)
- Help them find appropriate places and times for decision-making, and protect these
- Remove the thornier obstacles that may get in the way of implementation
- Help other adults understand the phenomenon of child-led governance to help the children’s parliament have real effects and experience success
