---
title: Notes for a Sixteenth
author: Mehrzad Esfandiari
---
At this age a person is exquisitely capable — hugely idealistic — feels they are The One. This calls for a structured transformation over a wide time horizon. 

The youth embodies: raw, exaggerated, energetic idealism; a felt sense of profound calling; a knowing of their own soul’s hidden greatness.

Purpose of the Sixteenth shall therefore prepare them for grand new privileges:

- The new adult shall now have the privilege of making a serious offering to the taux (Cypher holds special space for numies by new Sixteens) 
- The adult now has power to create new parliamentary Circles and train Ministers
- Wandeljahr: an improvisational year of discovery, transformation 

(Expect they shall continue to experience inner tumult, turbulence, spoken or not; the interface between youth and the world is evolving — at times distressed — a site of intensity; the ritual must succeed in honoring the youth’s Face)  

THEREFORE: 

Ritual form:

- Preparation: The vow. One year without looking at a mirror. Peers, friends become their mirror. Precise practices in core filaments entanglement (intellectual/creative/real ancestors); cultivating an ally for new and future responsibilities 
- Culminates in solitary camp Ancestors’ Campus: entanglement with ensouled elder (need a system to help them select which Tree)
- The youth’s Face is revealed to him in the mirror rites; his is honored with a presentation of handcrafted gifts from friends with incantation:
	- “I see you”
	- “I am here
- Next, departure to wander in vehicle of choice

Qq: Confer with Avir on supporting those who choose to exit via balloon
