#### [[superskin-skill-cloaking|Cloaking]]

The hallmark of a great raiment is a continuous protective superskin: a cloak. Even if your raiment is robust, well-nourished, and exuberant, it takes work to cloak, and like everything else, it takes practice! 

Summoning your filaments to cloak and maintaining it even for a few minutes actually requires a nontrivial expenditure of energy. It takes electrical energy to pull the filaments down into a cascade and to lightly bond the outermost filaments to one another; and it takes radiant energy to hold the boundary, keeping unwanted substances (clutter, noise, temptation, bugs) out of it. Building this capacity is a bit like building muscle. 
#### [[superskin-skill-billowing|Billowing]]

Can you remember a time when you were entranced into a timeless flow state by the presence of another person? If so, you have probably been in their billowing. 

When a person becomes very practiced at cloaking, their light but strong filaments may actually grow in dimension. Slowly, with practice, they can build the capacity to include other people within the scope of their cloak. Thus their body creates a contagious attentive attuned space. It’s experienced as a soft and contentless charisma. What’s actually happening on a molecular level is a simple billowing. Mystics, singers, and improvisers are professional billowers. They are adept at alchemizing intensely concentrated individual emotion into a collective protective force-field.  
#### [[splay-skill-entanglement|Entanglement]]

Entanglement requires the development of fine-motor skills for the tendres. 

When you entangle with another, the exchange of nutrients between tendres invigorates them, and so does the exercise. Over time and with practice, each entangled tendre slowly grows, thickens, and becomes more elastic, bringing more structural integrity to the entangled unit.
