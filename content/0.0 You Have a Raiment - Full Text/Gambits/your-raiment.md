---
title: Your raiment
---
This is an exercise taken directly from raiment reading groups that you can try. It is pretty simple:   

Draw yourself where you are right now, and draw your whole raiment, the way you think it exactly is right now. Be sure to include your nimbus, your superskin, and your splay with all its tendres. 

Write down ten words that describe the health and wellness condition of your raiment at this moment.
