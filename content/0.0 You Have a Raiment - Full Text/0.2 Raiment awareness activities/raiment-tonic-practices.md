title: Raiment-tonic practices
---
Notwithstanding Fone and all the challenges it brings, our era is actually quite a felicitous one when it comes to raiment wellbeing! This is in large part thanks to traditions of interactive somatics, organized by various interactive somatics guilds.

Interactive somatics practices provide a fertile ground for raiment health by creating a socially-reinforced methodology for refining critical superskin and splay skills. These whole-bodied attention and improvisation practices challenge and refine the entire raiment through the way they invite attention and generosity at three scales simultaneously: the individual, the pair, and the small group.

There are many, many different traditions of interpersonal somatics, each of which is enough to merit an entire text of its own. In our bioregion the most prominent and popular in this family of practices goes by the name “tango” (from “entangle” obviously).  This tradition usually happens in ritual spaces and takes shape according to musical containers created by divinely unhinged shamans.
