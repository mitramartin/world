---
title: O To A Child’s Raiment
author: Sousana Esfandiari
source: Sousana Esfandiari's personal papers
---
Slow yourself, loud man, slow yourself in the space of Her Sacred Raiment

Slow yourself that you may apprehend this organ of mystery of your own child

See it in original bloom, see the roots of the Great Vessels, see her whole body 

And feel the praising arise

The newborn is here and her raiment emblazons us in awe. 

O loud man, heaven help us, quiet your soul

That you may see Her Many-Splendored Raiment

Do you see her encloaked by a galaxy of sensate pores?

Do you see them as they inhale the soft fascination of your good planet? 

Do you feel your own goodness as you watch her nourish herself on the moonshine, the birdsong?   

Lucky, loud man, father, you will witness her as she learns to dance with its folds

As she discovers how to see its silent glimmers

To thread its gauge with lemmas and prophecies

You will be part of her saga as she learns to listen to its stored songs

As she discovers its spunk, its tugs, how it beckons her to where she needs to be 

Despite your voice, despite the loudness of your house

In time will her raiment let you see it change? Will you honor its great moultings and find the mood with which to embrace your child each time she becomes new? 

O father, I am not one to beg. But I beg you, today, for I am touched by the witness of her arrival: in this lifetime, let her sit and quietly listen to raiment. Build ye a whole home that centers its haleness. For her discerning raiment will protect her, as you honor it. 

Let her be with it in a quiet alcove. Let her paint with red paint until she is done.
