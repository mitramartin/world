**Welcome to ENOF!** 

We are an unincorporated sometimes-accessible township where the imaginal world is so convincingly rendered and skilfully overlaid with the material world as to be functionally indistinguishable from it. It is the not-yet-stabilized more beautiful world that our hearts know is here already. It is the place raiments tesseract into and out of, for certain very sophisticated entanglements. It’s also where all numies in every stage of progress are stored. 

We have been called “the fuzzy front end” to “neverland” to “the garden of delights.” It is where we go when we have had enough of the old ways. It is NOT to be confused with Fone. ENOF is the 100% inverse of Fone. Because in Fone everything is second or thirdhand but ENOF is by definition the place where everything is new.
