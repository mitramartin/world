**Diagnosis: 

1. Ungrounded. 
	- Provide opportunities to practice sockfoot and develop awareness of whole foot including corners. One foot at time. Explore varieties of footwear. Explore lower body shapes to develop precision and stop confusion, lostness, scatteredness. 

2. Controlling. 
	- Prescribe suite of solo musical meditations. Focus on vocal tangazos from mid 1940s, introducing flow state and heart-opening and reducing irritability, frustration. 

3. Reacting non-strategically and compulsively to things that happen.  
	- Random stillness practice and daily mirroring practice with a senpai will be helpful and help avoid impatience. Consider introducing oracle work to recreate missed Twelfth. Prioritize musical texts with obvious rhythmic features. 

4. Random content creation, expressive of an ongoing inner chatter. 
	- Raiment mirroring and matching while facing skilled partner will likely provide an ecology for selective refinement of raw ideation and thus reduce self-absorption.

*Summary:* Penelope’s raiment is largely sound, but pale, unmuscular, and bluntly acquisitive. Build capacity and sensitivity through a diet of safe, diverse and devoted entanglements.
