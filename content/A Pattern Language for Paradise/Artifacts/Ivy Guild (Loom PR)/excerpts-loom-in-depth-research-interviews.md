PROMPT: *"What is Fone?"*

VERBATIMS: 

“I heard that Fone started out as a sort of mechanical, electrical, person-built kind of dream world that you could actually go into when you weren’t asleep, and see your friends.” - Cora, 9

“Fone, I guess, is a place where you learn to argue about why some things are the same as other things. You learn how to show that things are equal to the things they are not.” - Penelope, 15, Ivy Guild matriculant 

“For me mostly it’s a place to get things done without actually doing anything, and get money that’s not really money but works the same. It’s kind of a big daydream. But there’s nothing better to do. And the money does work the same way, even if it doesn’t have the same feel.” - Sharon, 17, Stern Guild fellow

“For me, Fone the place you go when you need for two things to be the same while still being different. You say, ‘life is a circus,’ or ‘the girl is an angel’ or ‘this house is a hellhole,’ and in all those cases the equivalence kind of breaks your brain. Doesn’t make any sense. But it also creates a reality that needs a home, and guess what? It fits in Fone. In Fone the girl is an angel while also being not an angel. In Fone the house is indeed a hellhole and also your house. And your life can be a circus too, which it is, while also not being one.” - Frank, 47, opthamologist
