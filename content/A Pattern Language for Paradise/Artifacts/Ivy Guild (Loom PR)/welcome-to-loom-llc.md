We are an edtech company. We are proud to provide free education to all, funded by targeted advertising. 

Our focus is provisioning families with cheap or free smarthole hardware, Fone Callers, as well as fully individualized, gamified content/curriculum inside of Fone through which children can collect our proprietary experience point (XP) Hearts. 

Fone Callers are small, portable button pads that allow the user to instantaneously summon a personal portal directly beneath them. The portal arrives at the foot of the person who called it, where it temporarily melts the ground beneath it into a Fone entry-point. 

Our clients have the opportunity to purchase high quality advertising natively embedded into Loom educational content. We can consult with you to turn your business goals into child-friendly branded levels, quizzes, challenges and side-quests; hypercasual edugames that unlock coupons or offers; playable STEM interstitials that reward engagement with access to custom branded experiences; customization menus that allow learners to sport your brand’s digital goods, skins, tools, or avatars; atmospheric in-Fone classrooms, campuses, and thinking gardens featuring your brand colors and messaging; rigorous and engaging offerwalls between learning modules; fully clickable and shoppable interactive textbooks; and of course fully sponsored degree programs. We are able to embed any interactive asset from your mascot to your CEO into a grade level. Our educational advisors can assist with matching core curriculum elements to your company’s marketing needs. 

Brand awareness and loyalty skyrocket when your K-12 target audience are exposed to early, native sponsorships embedded in high-trust educational content, and this of course can translate easily to lifelong consumption.
