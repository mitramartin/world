title: Fone’s Famous Cracks
author: Audra Tickell

---
People used to think that Fone cracks were caused by Fone being dropped, but now we know they are created intentionally from the inside as part of a transit network. It connects Fone backwards and forwards to ENOF, so there’s easy egress for lost Fone explorers.  

Here is what I learned about Cracks during my Persephone week before my 12th, mostly from interviewing Bettina. Some famous cracks include the crack beneath the mineral watercourse at the very bottom of the semi-subterranean musical university (Bettina’s Own Crazing); and, a crack at the outskirts of Dis used by lost travelers in emergency conditions (Chartres Portal).  

One giant uncracked fault line is Deep Thankless, a subduction zone that extends from the regions of bland Ingratitude all the way into Dis. The fault runs directly under Fone’s fiat incarceration complex, a continent-sized facility where Loom’s Great Fiat is encrypted. 

Cracks can happen in a lot of ways. Most Fone cracks as a result of the percussive music that Spiders and their allies play deep inside of Fone along fault lines. An interesting kind of crack takes place when someone’s Fone hole is open and unguarded, and a storied object is hurled into the gaping hole.   

For instance, let’s say you used your Caller to summon your Fone hole and open it up, and then you throw a numinous item into it. Any kind of Thing of Beauty will work, such as an ancient harmonica or an origami narwhal. On impact, the thing will create a Crack that has transport qualities commensurate with its potency. 

Examples: The atmospheric river pouring just beside the Infinite Scroll, and the waterfall connecting the Cringey Falls to the little trickling creek out near tauxine grazing lands, are examples of cracks caused by such unusual objects.
