title: Polyparenting writeup
author: Sousana Esfandiari

In Meander, polyparenting is a mindset as well as a specific practice. The mindset is a general orientation around a duty of care toward young people and their raiment. Adults (both parents and non) see themselves as generally in the service of youth they encounter, holding an attitude of respect, curiosity, and helpfulness. The knowledge that fellow Meanderers enjoin to this mindset is liberating for everyone. When the whole community functions as a loving parent, a child can enjoy many freedoms and a parent can enjoy peace of mind. 

Yet, not all parenting needs are met by this general orientation. Parents often have the need to cultivate a specific culture, practice, or value system in their families which requires more curated influences and role models. Parenting has always been a practice of world-building, and in these times it is no different. This has given rise to experiments with the more specific practice of polyparenting: creating a Polyparent Cacophony.

As it is expressed currently, when they are pregnant, a child’s parents parents will curate a group of up to 12 individuals who enter into an explicit contract to collectively parent the new human being from birth until the child’s festival of new teeth (approximately 7 years.) This timeframe was chosen since it is decisively a critical period, during which time neural circuitry that underpins future outcomes is developed. It is also a time when parents are most in need of committed, values-aligned help they can count on.  

Like many Meander patterns, polyparenting is a work-in-progress and not without its frictions.
