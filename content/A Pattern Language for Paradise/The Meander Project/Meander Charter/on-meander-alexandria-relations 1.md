As this goes to publication, we are still really just at the very beginning of the grand pan-raiment project. Most of those involved expect it to take at least until the year 2087, with visible, soft, deity-facilitated tipping points taking place in the late 70s. The growth of interpersonal somatics laboratories including networks of aligned guilds will of course play a critical role. But there is also a lot of much harder stuff to work out in this fuzzy front end of the endeavor. 

As pointed out in the sidebar Meander: A History, 

	“The most difficult issues Meander thought leaders had to navigate were relations with neighboring Alexandria. These were often at the heart of the General Circle’s deliberations. As the Meander project unfolded, some of its founders advocated for isolationism, whereas others sought a more interactive approach.”

This paragraph alludes to the fraught and frightful times we find ourselves in now. Of course it would be great if we could just interweave everyone across both Meander and Alexandria into nice happy Integers but it turns out to not be so easy to do that while you are also trying to essentially establish an entire new social order from scratch. 

Opportunities for Integer-building between Meanderers and Alexandrians have been scarce, and most of the Meander founders are still basically in the early stages of essentially starting a small nation state, or network society if you prefer, and they simply do not have the bandwidth to take on this task. Even despite their adherence to it as a goal. There is always just too much to do!

Other cultural and physical things also get in the way: lack of transportation infrastructure connecting the two settlements; Alexandria’s prestige-orientation fueled by Loom, XP Arcades, and highly competitive guild placement system; and of course the two settlements’ diametrically different relations with fiats. But of course the most decisive obstacle is Alexandrians’ orientation to raiment. Since most doubt its existence, and some aggressively deny it, the Meander project is illegible to Alexandrians. 

However, inspired by material accessed through their core filaments during milestone birthday rites, several young and ambitious members of Meander’s youth with sturdy raiment have established several endeavors related to forging relations with Alexandria, and the results of these Investigations have not yet fruited.
