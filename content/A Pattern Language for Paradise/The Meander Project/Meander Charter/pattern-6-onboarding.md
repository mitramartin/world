Meander’s founding walked a fine line between techno-caution and techno-optimism. The founders (many of whom as you will remember had a background as technocrats themselves) wanted their settlement and their children to enjoy the benefits of new technology but to be shielded from its invasive and unsavory side. 

So, we studied the history of science/tech and identified a false dichotomy which pitted a focus on limiting access to new technologies against a total laissez-faire approach. We didn’t quite jibe with either, knowing one can neither transform nor sabotage anything you have not immersed yourself in. Although we subscribed to the precautionary principle, we also believed that activating it would require a missing piece: a program of extremely high quality ecological literacy education, which we called “onboarding.” The goal was to help human bodies sanely and healthfully metabolize new technologies, and we applied it to our Fone approach.
