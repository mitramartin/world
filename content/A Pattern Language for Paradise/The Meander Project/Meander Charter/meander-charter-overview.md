## The founding of Meander

#### Welcome 

Thank you for your interest in our experiment in establishing a raiment-friendly [zome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zome_(architecture)). A bit about us: we were members of the pattern language collective. Over the course of the Decade of Raiment Ravishing when the [[Invitation to Raiment Wellness Collective|collective]]’s research was in full foment, several other Alexandria neighbors joined our orbit and together we incubated an idea for collectively establishing a new experimental settlement to battle-test some of the [[0.004 notes-from-meanders-pan-raimentism-experiments|pan-raiment patterns]]. We have gathered several of the foundational documents and artifacts here. We are glad to share them here with anyone interested. 

Sincerely, Mehrzad and Sousana Esfandiari
