title: The Tango Guild Origin Story
author: Audra Tickell
source: Tango Guild archives
---
How did the tango church start you ask? Oh, wait, they hate when we call it that. The tango guild, the tango guild. 

Well. Mom told me it was started by a traveling salesman. He changed people when he embraced them, and he left all kinds of confusion in his wake, that took years to untangle, and the untangling was the tango.   

I gather he was a perplexed and perplexing guy. He was passionate, confident, partially competent. He had a stutter and a limp and this unearthly charisma. Very much the music man, seducer, thief, friend, old boy. He came around Alexandria a few times on his tours and each time he left people more befuddled and angsty and electrified. On his last tour, he took everyone downstairs, deep into Fone, into a cavernous subterranean MP3 grotto the size of twenty aircraft carriers, and in an demented ceremony he bequeathed the entire crystal catacombs to the forbears our little club. People are still processing that. 

The club, because that was all it was at that point, attracted misfits, retired actors, lonely hearts, pixie types, but also brainiacs used to the rigors of solving intractable problems, which the tango definitely was. As it grew it got the attention of decentralized organization development folks. They tended to be mellow, quiet, deep-waters types, and sometimes spoke in assertively elaborate and slightly anachronistic ways, for comical affect. But when they took the floor their tangos were fire.   

I got involved as a Tango Bear after my Sixteenth. Ever since I was a teenager I had liked hanging out with them. It was always really comfortable and there were always interesting conversations. They listened really well and asked me good questions and listened more, and then asked more detailed questions. That was so new. And I know instinctively on some level that being in the mix there was having a good effect on my own awful experiences as a little kid in Fone.
