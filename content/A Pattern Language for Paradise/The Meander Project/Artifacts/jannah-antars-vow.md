title: Jannah Antar's Vow
author: Jannah Antar
---
I was home from school sick the day the shooting happened.   

Well, I wasn’t really sick, but my hands were super itchy and the wounds were weepy because I had scratched them that night. I knew it would be weird to be around kids at school. They would stare or whisper or maybe be super polite and nice, which was weird too. 

Anyway school would be pointless. I mean, I wouldn’t tell mom that. But I was already two, sometimes three problem sets ahead in every class, and the teachers didn’t pay attention to the kids who were ahead.   

One time I was a little stumped on a question and I asked the teacher a question about it and when she “explained” it to me she was totally wrong. I mean, 100%, in every way. And the wrong shape her wrong mouth made when she opened it was so annoying. including her extremely superior mood as she broke everything down in complete wrongitude. I worked out the whole thing and figured out the answer myself and then checked it in the back and I was right. I’m used to that now. Sitting in a cold quiet classroom with a useless teacher working on a workbook that nobody else understands is pretty much equal to doing the same thing in my own slightly warmer house, except that in my house nobody stares at my hands. 

So you see. School was a pretty stupid place. I just needed to get through it fast. If nobody bothered me with dumb stuff maybe I could get through it all a year, two years faster. 

So back to the morning of the shooting. That morning I had told Mom I wasn’t feeling well. She looked at my eyes. She made me an oatmeal and then headed out to review an amicus. I timed myself while I did another section, then I graded it. I never look at the answers til I’m done, honor code. I really got every question right just on my own. Just a few years of problem sets lay between me and Ivy Guild, which was freedom. 

I don’t like to think about what happened and anyway all the details are all documented elsewhere if people with sick minds want to look into them. I couldn’t concentrate on work because of all the sirens. So many sirens. My hands wept and wept and were the color of sirens. I couldn’t get home til late. Traffic had been stopped everywhere while the emergency vehicles were getting to places with negotiators and law enforcement and all the useless emergency medical. 

I don’t remember how I learned that Rainer’s folks had been killed. We weren’t really communicators in our family. I think I overheard Mom telling Dad that his school had had no survivors, and then there was the mass funeral. And then Rainer came to live with us. 

So. That was the day I vowed that I would put an end to school. School anyway was always a sick place.  

And you know what? I love working at Loom. We are solving interesting and important problems and we’re doing more than you can possibly know. I know Loom isn’t popular and it’s fashionable to poke holes at Fone. Sure, I bet you can make something better than XP Arcades you have infinite time and money. Create your own damn Bling School. Go for it. But also, try to remember how awful school can be, people. I’m just trying to save children from that thing. I will save children from school. And come on, what is really so evil about pizza and snacks and collecting points?
