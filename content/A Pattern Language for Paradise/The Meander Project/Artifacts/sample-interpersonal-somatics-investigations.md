ENTANGLEMENT Investigation 3.2: IF your presence evokes arousal in me, how shall I prepare myself such that I can stand in front of you and calmly be who I am? 

BILLOWING Investigation #5.7: IF our attention is most capacious at the beginning of a thing, how shall we evoke the experience of beginningness throughout a process that has duration? 

ENTANGLEMENT Investigation 6.7: IF music makes us partly liquid, yet we still want to build a structure together, then how shall we determine which parts of you, and which parts of me, will remain solid? 

ENTANGLEMENT Investigation 9.1: IF I become someone new each time I stand in front of you, and you become someone new each time you stand in front of me, how do we benefit from our shared history? 

ENTANGLEMENT Investigation 10.3: IF there are ten thousand possible ways of beginning together, and some are easy, and some are medium, and some are difficult, and we want to master all of them, how shall we approach the problem? 

BILLOWING Investigation #6.1: IF a grouping of people who profess a culture of liberation consistently have the effect on us such that we feel unfree, but we cannot decipher that this is so, what dimensions might be underexplored? 

BILLOWING Investigation #8.8: IF a certain kind of gathering brings out habitual patterns in us, how shall we prepare ourselves such that we can be honest while presenting at the gathering?
