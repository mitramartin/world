title: Sousana’s original Exorcism of the Decimals from the Tickell home
author: Sousana Esfandiari

YOU. I have seen you disorganize this child’s new RAIMENT.

I have seen you disorganize her tender integrity with your carelessness, your obliviousness, your demands for attention. 

I have seen you decimate her little strivings, in piousness and sanctimony, concealing your own crisis of self-worth. 

I have seen you in your ignorance, your laziness, your smug self-importance, I have seen you systematically occlude her tiny ways of attempting to fulfill the promise she made to herself when she came here. 

I have seen you quietly institutionalize intrusions that make no sense to anyone, that catastrophically decimate her wellspring of self, under the slickest of cultural sanctions. There is no room for you here. 

BE GONE.
