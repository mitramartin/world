title: The immutable laws of the Tango operating system
author: Dante Quattrochi
source: Tango Guild archives
---
I find it quite meaningful that the operating system we now call Tango emerged where it did at the time when it did. 

While world wars fueled by primitive thinking raged on the other side of the planet, an embodied operating system of pure love was unfolding on the silver shores of Buenos Aires and Montevideo.

It started with mutual aid societies made of artists who were immigrants and exploited and enslaved peoples from all over the world, like Africa, Europe, and the Carribean. Their only shared language was the sacred music of their ancestors. Their inter-musical investigation went extremely deep, unlocked an extremely refined kind of listening, and thus gifted humanity with some of the most rich, powerful, evocative music ever to exist. Out of decades of exploration and improvisation came a whole new world musical idiom that holds many of the same properties as psychedelics. This body of work inspired human hearts to embrace one another and dance in large numbers while maintaining an attitude of exquisite tenderness towards all creation.[^1]

Here are the elements of the Argentinian/Uruguayan-born Tango operating system that these amazing ancestors booted up for us: 

1. CORAZÓN: You take yourself, your inner life, and the energy of your heart seriously. 
2. PISTA: You hold a piece of the earth as a sacred sanctuary for connection.
3. CABECEO: You choose one another freely. 
4. ENTREGA: You commit your whole self to the all-to-short experience of engaging with another human being.
5. ABRAZO: You hold your partner with infinite care and respect. 
6. SALIDA: You are willing to exit conventional reality and enter a new dimension, together. 
7. SISTEMA: You draw on a basic set of designs that provides just enough constraint to invite freedom. 
8. TANDA: You attend your partner lovingly and respectfully for a contained amount of linear time. 
9. RONDA: You do it in a space where everyone else is also doing it with great care for one another. 
10. BARRIO: You care for your local community and bring positive vibes.  
11. INVESTIGACIÓN: You search for ways to deepen and expand the set of joyous possibilities together with peers and partners.
12. DUENDE: You court access to the realm of faerie through surrender to the portal of music made explicitly for the practice of explosively beautiful not-kissing.

[^1]: Experts now refer to the active creative ecosystem that D. Quattrochi alludes to here technically as a, lunar efflorescence of numies (león).
