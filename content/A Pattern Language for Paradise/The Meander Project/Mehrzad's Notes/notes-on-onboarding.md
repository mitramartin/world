Having done some preliminary investigation of Fone, we can verify that there is enough danger to merit caution. We encourage an attitude of erring, ever so slightly, on the side of prudence and hereby entreat you to activate the precautionary principle for Meander. 

This memo offers the following general orientation of what that would look like, in terms of our behaviors regarding Fone as it relates to Meander’s youngest people. 

1. The landscape of Fone is full of many interesting, uncatalogued species. Some may have untoward effects on raiment, especially tender raiment. Therefore **portals should be managed and supervised, especially for the very small**. (Note that Fone is *not yet scaffolded* with any age-based safety measures.) 
2. In addition, and not contradictorily, we encourage leaning into dialogue. As nobody fully understands Fone yet, and its terrain is changing by the day, we are not yet ready to establish curriculum, or even soft norms documentation. We encourage polyparents across the settlement to **create a culture of inquiry with regards to Fone tourism**, orally narrating their reflections, questions, and experiences to children in attendance. We encourage the naming and unpacking of value tensions. This will provide a basis for resonance and critical thinking together. 
3. Third, we believe Fone venturing will be healthiest for all in the larger context of **embodied practice**. Speak with your coach about how you might establish some good habits that support wellness. There are theories that Fone can disrupt circadian and infradian cycles; scheduling your day and timeboxing your Fone excursions may be more important than you’d think. Ensuring your Fone serves your body’s well being and mental health will ensure we maintain the sound culture for our children — which as we all remember is Meander’s reason for being. 

These are attitudes that we can all adopt right now, that will protect our vision for Meander and give us time to carry out further research, which will help us develop a deeper understanding of what types of programs and interventions would be appropriate to invest in vis-à-vis our new subterranean neighboring community Fone. 

## Long-term investments

Over the long term we envision that it will be necessary for children to engage in an extended and intentional onboarding period with multiple carefully designed phases, each with a ritual language to heighten the attentiveness with which they engage their Fone. We propose developing the following elements over the next 3-5 years:  

**Phase 1 (Ages 4-6):** Fone Mentors. At the age of 4, the child may be assigned 3-4 trained Fone Mentors. These are adults trusted by their parents, with whom the child may venture into Fone.

**Phase 2 (Age 7-11):** Onboarding. At the child’s Seventh Festival, we hold an unboxing ceremony wherein they receive their own Fone caller. At this time their Onboarding begins. They are assigned to a mixed-age peer cohort who will guide work with them to establish a basic plan for the exploration of Fone.

**Phase 3 (Age 12-15):** Fone Quest. To prepare for the child’s Twelfth, they are required to spend a week within Fone with two specific tasks: to capture their Monster, and to find their Thing of Beauty. Encamping for an extended period with their cohort in Fone enables them to practice Fone skills in a supported environment with hidden allies, and to bring forward both the gleaming and shadow sides of their Fone during their Twelfth. Once they complete their Quest they may step into their roles as Onboarding guides. 

**Phase 4 (Age 16+):** Onboarding Leader. The youth is now fully onboarded and can step into their role leading an integer and evolving this program. 

N.B.: These investments will require careful planning and collaboration and many years to bring to fruition, and likely will even require developing a funding model. Fortunately we can draw on many resources and subject matter experts among the founders community. Rheba has dedicated her career to onboarding consulting, and Sousana has a deep and tapestraic understanding of underworld culture. We propose forming an ad-hoc circle to begin activation and will add this to the agenda of the next General Circle meeting. We welcome your input and involvement in this endeavor as it is a great big huge massive undertaking.
