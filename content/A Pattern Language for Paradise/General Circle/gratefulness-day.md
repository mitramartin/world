---
title: Gratefulness Day
author: Sousana Esfandiari
source: Meander General Circle archives
---
Today is the first Gratefulness Day that the children will be running on their own. They have a lot to do. They have to prepare a numinous weather. They have to work the soils with an amendment of benevolence. Sprinklings of well chosen words that make the intimate act of praising break through its shell and fight its way onto the page. 

Most of the deliberation relates to the Prompt. They will pluck three or four plummy prompts from all their long walks into ENOF. They will taste these very carefully, They will place the rosiest best-ripened ones on a tray, and they will serve these to all of Meander. That will be how Gratefulness Day begins.  

Every thank-you note written that day is placed in a time-capsule to be forgotten until it is extracted. Every song of appreciation packaged for the delight of a future self. That waiting-period, that delayed obfuscated receipt, that crazy quirk in the midst of things…

Yes, people always wonder about that. Does it all even out? Do you get as much as you give? Who even knows? By the time the notes are delivered nobody even cares, they are charms, delights, punch lines, fortune cookies. What matters more is the quality of the ritual. And the cadence. 

The children intuit it. That the heartfeltness of this ritual is the engine of the SlowXP scheme, and nothing else. It’s not what’s on the blockchain. Those digits don’t matter, they are a foil, there to confound titans of industry and other would-be colonizers. The children know that this matters and matters and matters, it matters even if the words of gratefulness are never even read by anyone. This is the spring pump that pulls the lifewater up from those cold blue underground rivers so it can quench us. And it does. It really does.
